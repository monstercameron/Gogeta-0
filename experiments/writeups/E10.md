## What it will test, in plain English

> **Is our yardstick straight?** Every result so far compares our models against [E0](E0.html), a standard design we wrote ourselves and trained once, with a learning rate (how big a step the model takes each update) that nobody tuned. If E0 was accidentally handicapped, our new designs look better than they are.
>
> E10 replaces it with a reference we didn't write: Hugging Face's standard **Llama** model, built at exactly E0's size (13.77M parameters, the same arithmetic per token). It gets its own tuned learning rate and is trained **3 times with different random seeds**, so we can see how much results wobble by chance. From then on, a new design only "wins" if it beats E10 by more than that wobble.

## What we'll run

- **Learning-rate tuning (check K7):** short runs (20M tokens) at four learning rates for E10 and for our best design, [E1](E1.html). Each model gets the rate that scores best, picked by a rule written down before the runs.
- **Replication:** E10 and E1 at their tuned rates, 3 seeds each, 200M tokens.
- **Parity:** if E10's tuned rate differs from the one E0 used, E0 is re-run at E10's rate. That tells us whether our own dense model was a fair stand-in.

The predictions and pass criteria were written before any run, in the [registry](https://github.com/monstercameron/Gogeta-0/blob/main/experiments/experiments.json) and in `experiments/checks/PREDICTIONS.md`.
