# SQ365: ternary threshold sensitivity on the 200M F2 checkpoints

**Status:** done (descriptive, not registered) · **Verdict:** a 5%-of-scale Gaussian jitter of the latent weights costs the trained ternary cells +1.39% (001) and +2.63% (111) bits/char, 6-12x the +0.23% / +0.21% it costs their full-precision counterparts (000 / 100); the ternary cells also have 1.8-2.4x more weights within 0.05 scale of a rounding threshold (10.5% / 14.1% vs 6.0%) · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq365_ternary_jitter.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq365.json`

## Method
Cells 001 (dense, ternary), 111 (full fusion, ternary) and the controls 000, 100 (full precision, same layers), best LR, seeds 0-2. For every BitLinear layer (all `make_linear` layers; none are full precision in these cells) the latent weight w gets Gaussian noise of std 0.05 x absmean(w) of that layer; 3 independent draws (generator seeds 1000-1002); the model requantises inside its forward as in training (scale recomputed from the jittered weights). Metric: aggregate bits/char on the SQ339 300-doc clean80 subset, fp32 CPU (GPU agreement 0.015%); penalty = 100 x (jittered / base - 1), mean over the 3 draws, then mean +- sd over seeds. Near-threshold share = fraction of weights with | |w|/s - 0.5 | < 0.05 (the zone where noise of 0.05 scale can flip the ternary code); flip share = fraction of codes that actually change under draw 0 (round(w/s) clamped to [-1, 1]).

## Result (n = 3 seeds)
| cell | penalty under jitter (mean, sd; per seed) | near-threshold share | codes flipped by one draw |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 ternary dense | +1.39% (sd 0.38; +0.96, +1.52, +1.69) | 10.53% | 4.10% |
| 111 ternary fusion | +2.63% (sd 0.74; +2.10, +3.48, +2.30) | 14.09% | 5.47% |
| 000 dense fp | +0.23% (sd 0.02) | 5.96% | 2.38% |
| 100 GDN fp | +0.21% (sd 0.003) | 5.95% | 2.37% |
(per-seed numbers in the JSON; the seed 1 fusion run is the most fragile, +3.5%.)

## Reading and caveats
- Trained ternary models are markedly more sensitive to small latent-weight noise than the same architectures in full precision, and the fused ternary model more than the dense ternary one: consistent with weights piled up against rounding boundaries (near-threshold 10-14% against 6% for the fp cells, which equals what a smooth bell-shaped weight distribution gives). It does not by itself say that the ternary cost is caused by this fragility, only that the trained models sit nearer boundaries.
- The penalty is a reaction to eval-time perturbation of the latent weights, not training noise; mapping 5% of scale to any actual optimiser or precision noise is an assumption. One noise level only (5%); no dose-response. 300 docs, n = 3 seeds, descriptive.
- Controls apply noise to weights that were never quantised, so their small penalty is the generic sensitivity to 5% relative weight noise.
