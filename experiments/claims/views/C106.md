- Demonstrated 2026-11-23 18:40 (experiments/checks/sandbox_kill_cleanup.py, saved output test-runs/2026-11-23-sandbox-kill-cleanup-1840.txt): a sandboxed snippet whose child calls setsid survives the timeout's process-group SIGKILL (as nobody), and the harness then blocks in proc.communicate() with no timeout (post_training/rewards.py:495, after killpg) until that process exits. So one generated sample can stall a reward or eval worker indefinitely and leave a process running. Same-group children and busy loops are killed within the 1 s timeout, and no mount leaks to the host. Fix: SQ61's PID namespace (unshare -p -f --kill-child) plus a bounded communicate after the kill.
