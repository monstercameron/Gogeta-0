# Feasibility review and final-training notes (GPT-6.2 Astra, 2026-11-22 23:43)

**Decision: advance the unlooped G-1B hybrid to an inference prototype and a real-code scaling pilot. Hold the E61 training rental.** At 14M parameters, the hybrid comes close enough to a tuned Llama that a serving advantage could justify choosing it. The strongest evidence favors testing distinct depth before adding shared passes. Neither the quality of the full fusion nor its batch-1 speed on a 24 GB card has been demonstrated. [C5, C6, C197](../experiments/claims-status.md) · [E61 plan, §§4–6](final-model-plan.md)

The proposed 1.226B model comfortably fits the **weight and persistent-cache** budget on paper. The decisions that could change the purchase are how much clean Python survives filtering, what the model learns from repeated exposure, whether cached generation is exact and fast on both target cards, and how many tokens a synchronized 5090 host processes per billed hour. [Final-model plan](final-model-plan.md) · [Corpus-v3 plan](research-corpus-v3.md) · [Current trainer](../gogeta/train.py)

## 2. Architecture feasibility, ingredient by ingredient

### GDN, sliding windows and occasional full attention — **keep as the candidate backbone**

- **Evidence.** The seven-seed E1/E10 comparison makes the quality trade plausible. The short v2 decomposition also found 0.4342 bpc for E1 versus 0.4646 after replacing GDN with attention, one seed. SQ214 found window 256 beat opening the windows to 1,024 by 1.30% at 20M tokens. These results justify retaining the hybrid for the pilot. [Phase 2b](../experiments/checks/results/phase2b_5090.json) · [DEVLOG v0.4.80](../DEVLOG.md) · [SQ214](../experiments/checks/results/sq_214.json)
- **At 1B.** [speculation] Four global-attention layers may be ample for average Python loss while missing a distant definition, import alias or repeated identifier. Dense-masked SWA could also erase the expected prefill advantage. G-1B has four global layers across 24 blocks, whereas the measured E1 had one across six. [C16/C193](../experiments/claims-status.md) · [Model implementation](../gogeta/model.py) · [Candidate shapes](final-model-plan.md)
- **Cheapest decision.** Score existing checkpoints by identifier reuse distance and controlled copy distance (SQ209/SQ240). Require a working attention positive control. Then run v3 R0/R1 against tuned E10, SQ293 for GQA, and SQ244/SQ294 for real 8k–32k quality and elapsed time. Keep E10 as the fallback if the hybrid loses meaningful capability without a measured serving benefit. [Side quests](../experiments/side-quests.md) · [v3 re-baselining](research-corpus-v3.md)

### Looped depth — **gate; use unshared depth by default**

- **Evidence.** Parameter sharing works mechanically (C10). Its quality per training FLOP is weak under E2's uniform pass count, residual carry and truncated backpropagation. The Inkling 2 **Mini** source review reports fixed three-pass recurrence, thicker outer layers, gated messages and restart from the prelude. Its reported matched-loss nonlooped comparator used 7.5% less training compute. No published ablation there isolates restart or gating. [C6/C7/C10/C197](../experiments/claims-status.md) · [Inkling 2 Mini §§2–4](research-inkling2-mini.md) · [Loop literature review](research-looped.md)
- **At 1B.** [speculation] A different recurrence may exploit more data, yet every pass still consumes compute and needs its own historical state. A parameter saving alone has little value when the unshared bf16 target already fits. The economic case would be equal-quality faster decoding or better quality at the same training spend. [Plan §§2.2, 3.1 and 6](final-model-plan.md)
- **Cheapest decision.** Read SQ266/SQ271 and LR-fair SQ282, then the 200M seed top-up SQ283. If warranted, test containment first (SQ273), then closed-gate restart (SQ268). Admit loops only on the plan's three-seed matched-FLOP quality gate, or within 1% quality with **measured ≥1.3×** self-speculative decoding. [Wave 6 registration](../experiments/checks/PREDICTIONS.md) · [Loop gate](final-model-plan.md)

## 3. What the 24 GB inference target actually constrains

**Parameter and cache arithmetic.** Recomputing the proposed GQA shape from the model's projection dimensions gives about **1,225,920,384 tied parameters** for G-1B, or 2.452 GB in bf16. The analogous S and X counts are about 0.311B and 3.331B. The current implementation has equal query and KV head counts, so these are counts for the proposed implementation. [Model dimensions](../gogeta/model.py) · [Plan §2.1](final-model-plan.md)

| Recomputed quantity for G-1B, batch 1 | Consequence |
|---|---|
| `4 global layers × 2 (K,V) × 4 KV heads × 128 × 2 bytes = 8,192 bytes/token` | 256 MiB at 32,768 tokens and exactly 1 GiB at 131,072. With the present 16-head MHA shape it would be 4 GiB at 131,072. GQA materially reduces long-context read traffic. [Plan §2.2 and SQ293](final-model-plan.md) |
| `8 SWA layers × 2 × 4 × 128 × 2 × 256 = 4 MiB` | Bounded storage requires an actual ring or equivalent implementation. SWA window 4,096 takes 64 MiB. Each repeated core visit needs a separate logical cache. [Plan §2.2](final-model-plan.md) · [Attention implementation](../gogeta/model.py) |
| `12 GDN layers × 16 heads × 128² × 4 bytes = 12 MiB` | The plan already budgets **fp32 state**. Eight extra rollback snapshots cost 96 MiB, plus live state, convolution tails and any draft state. Test drift over long decoding before considering lower state precision. [Plan §§2.2 and 5](final-model-plan.md) · [SQ70 test dimensions](../experiments/checks/results/sq_hw_fla_recurrent.json) |
| `21.5 GB usable − 6.66 GB X weights − 1.34 GB X KV − 1 GB workspace − fixed state` | At 128k, G-3B bf16 has about **12.4 GB** left under the plan's assumptions. Its “>15 GB to spare at every size and precision” sentence is incorrect. Both P and X still have ample persistent-memory headroom. [Plan §§1–2.3](final-model-plan.md) |

**Decode arithmetic.** At 4k on the 3090, `0.8 × 936 GB/s` divided by roughly 2.51 GB of weights, KV, ring and state traffic gives **about 298 tokens/s**. At 32k it gives about **272**. These reproduce the plan's bf16 bandwidth estimates. Achieved bandwidth, launch overlap, dequantization, graph pools and effective GQA reads remain unmeasured. The 450–600 int8 range is a prediction. [Plan §4 and SQ288](final-model-plan.md)

**Prefill arithmetic.** The advertised two seconds for 32k prices `2NT`, about 80.3 TFLOP. Four causal full-attention layers add about `2 × 4 × 2,048 × 32,768² = 17.6 TFLOP` for QK and AV under the model's counting convention. That gives **at least about 2.45 seconds** at the assumed 40 TFLOP/s, before SWA, GDN recurrence and overhead. Chunking controls peak memory but still reads prior context. [Model FLOP formula](../gogeta/model.py) · [Plan §4](final-model-plan.md)

**The current prefill path cannot substantiate even that estimate.** `Attention._mask` constructs a square integer distance tensor and caches a square Boolean mask per SWA layer and sequence length. At 32k the integer temporary alone is 8 GiB. `Rotary.forward` takes positions from zero, and `sample()` repeatedly forwards the truncated prefix. SQ275 bounds mask retention. SQ286/SQ294 need offset-aware RoPE, genuinely windowed kernels and chunked prefill. Measure peak allocated **and** reserved/process memory, time to first token, and steady decode separately. [Model](../gogeta/model.py) · [Sampler](../gogeta/train.py) · [SQ275 and SQ285–294](../experiments/side-quests.md)

**Speculative speed arithmetic needs a correction.** The plan derives a separate-drafter cost ratio of 0.15 from weight bytes. Its own bf16 sizes give `0.62/2.45 ≈ 0.253`; a launch floor cannot explain lowering that ratio. With acceptance 0.7 and three proposals, the simple idealized formula gives `(1 + 0.7 + 0.7² + 0.7³)/(1 + 3 × 0.253) ≈ 1.44×` before extra verify or rollback costs. Measure both step latencies, verify length and accepted tokens per round on generated code. [Plan §§4–5](final-model-plan.md) · [Speculation cost model](research-speculative-bytes.md)

**Losslessness is a state-management requirement.** Early passes in today's loop do not depend on total pass count, but the draft and verifier codas see different hidden states. Verify with the correct offset causal mask and handle rejection at every draft position. A pointer rewind cannot restore SWA entries already overwritten after a ring wraps. Stage speculative writes or retain overwritten slots, restore GDN and convolution state, then append the replacement token. Test wraparound, chunk boundaries, EOS and full acceptance against ordinary cached decoding. [FusionDance.forward](../gogeta/model.py) · [SQ286/SQ287/SQ296](final-model-plan.md)

Greedy token identity and sampling correctness are separate acceptance tests. For sampling, check the rejection rule and residual distribution against an enumerable target, then exercise randomized multi-step rejection paths. State which target precision defines exactness. Int8 speculation must reproduce that int8 target's distribution; its quality difference from bf16 belongs in SQ290. [C19](../experiments/claims/views/C19.md) · [Plan §5](final-model-plan.md)

**Hardware and useful context.** SQ220 demonstrated hd-64 chunk training on a 3090. C111 demonstrated hd-64 recurrent decoding on sm_121. Neither covers model-level decoding at hd 128 on sm_86 and sm_89. SQ285 should record the selected backend and parity on each card. SQ294 should test an explicit RoPE extension from trained 8k to 32k on long real code and retrieval controls. A 128k cache allocation establishes capacity only. Report bytes/s alongside tokens/s when SQ277 changes tokenizer compression. [SQ220](../experiments/devlogs/SQ220.md) · [C111](../experiments/claims/views/C111.md) · [Plan §§1, 6–7](final-model-plan.md)
