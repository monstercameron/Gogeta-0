# SQ349: syntax-validity proxy for the 200M F2 cells (low power)

**Status:** done (descriptive, not registered; a null here is weak) · **Verdict:** no functional penalty visible: 100% bracket validity in all three cells, indentation consistency 96.7% (000), 93.3% (100), 96.7% (111) over 60 greedy continuations each; the differences are 1-2 generations and far inside noise · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq349_syntax_proxy.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq349.json` (generated text stored)

## Method
20 clean80 val docs (random.Random(0), >= 400 tokens) cut at a line end after about half their length (prompt = at most the last 320 tokens, from a line start); each model (cells 000, 100, 111, best LR, seeds 0-2; 111 at loops = 4) continues with 64 greedy tokens (fp32 CPU, full forward per step). Checks on prompt + generation, no execution: (1) bracket_ok: no closer in the generated part mismatches the innermost open bracket (string and comment aware); (2) indent_ok: complete generated lines use the prompt's whitespace style, indents are multiples of the prompt's indent unit and never jump more than one unit deeper. Checker unit-tested on hand cases; positive control: the real continuations (first 200 chars) of the 14 prompts whose text is available pass both checks 14/14.

## Result (3 seeds x 20 prompts = 60 generations per cell)
| cell | bracket_ok | indent_ok | both |
|---|---|---|---|
| 000 dense | 100% | 96.7% | 96.7% |
| 100 GDN | 100% | 93.3% | 93.3% |
| 111 fusion | 100% | 96.7% | 96.7% |
Per-seed fractions (20 prompts each) are in the JSON.

## Reading and caveats
64 tokens is a short prefix, so unterminated brackets and strings are not penalised and the bracket check is nearly vacuous (ceiling 100% everywhere); a 3-point indent difference is 2 generations out of 60 (Wilson 95% CI half-width about 10 points). Prompts are shared across seeds and cells, generations are within-prompt dependent. Conclusion limited to: the bits/char penalties of the fusion cells (+30% at 111) do not show up as gross local syntax breakage in 64-token continuations. Not a functional (execution) test.
