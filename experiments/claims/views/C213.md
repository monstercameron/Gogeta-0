## Limitations

- Scale compression confound (SQ379 devlog, 'Reading and caveats'): out-of-corpus losses are 1.65-1.9 bits/char, 6-7x the in-corpus ones, so every relative effect is compressed toward 0 there. The causal reading 'memorisation capacity' is therefore not established. The attenuation is differential (the assembly gain keeps about two thirds of its size, -11.3% vs -16.7%, while the ternary cost goes to about 0), which uniform compression alone does not explain, but the hypothesis stays exploratory until SQ400 (seed variance, leave-one-package-out, a repo-held-out slice) and an absolute-difference readout separate the two.
