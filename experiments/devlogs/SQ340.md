# SQ340: fp32 vs bf16-emulated scoring of the 200M F2 cells

**Status:** done · **Verdict:** prediction held. bf16 rounding at the GDN cast points moves bits/char by at most 0.0035% in any cell (threshold: 1 point) and moves the seed-blocked interaction I by 0.0004 points (fp32 I = +6.36 points) · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq340_bf16_emulation.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq340.json`, registration `experiments/checks/PREDICTIONS.md` section SQ340 (committed in v0.5.3 before the run)

## Method
Cells 000, 100, 111 (best LR, seeds 0-2) plus the GDN-free cells 001 and 010 in fp32 (needed for I; they contain no GDN mixer, so the emulation does not touch them), 300 clean80 docs (SQ339 subset), fp32 CPU. Arms for cells 100 and 111: (a) fp32; (b) the registered arm: q, k, v and the mixer output rounded to bf16 (round trip) at the points where the GPU path casts; (c) sensitivity: (b) plus beta rounded to bf16 (the GPU path also casts beta). Emulation caveat: only the mixer inputs/outputs are rounded; the GPU run also uses bf16 autocast for all matmuls and the fla kernel's internal accumulation, which this does not reproduce. I = y111 - y100 - y010 - y001 + 2 y000 (log bpc), seed-blocked estimator of sq263.py.

## Result (n = 3 seeds)
- Shift = 100 x (bpc_b / bpc_a - 1): cell 100: +0.0006% (b), -0.0003% (c); cell 111: +0.0010% (b), +0.0035% (c); 95% CIs all include 0 (e.g. 111 (c): [-0.008, +0.015]). Dense cells have no GDN: shift 0 by construction.
- |shift(111) - shift(100)| = 0.0004 points (b) [CI -0.007, +0.008], 0.0038 points (c) [CI -0.011, +0.019]: far below the registered 1 point.
- I (log-points, seed-blocked, df 8, on these 300 docs): fp32 +6.36 [+4.67, +8.06]; (b) +6.36 [+4.67, +8.06]; (c) +6.37 [+4.67, +8.07]. Per-seed fp32 I: +8.68, +5.03, +5.38. Shift in I: +0.0004 (b), +0.0038 (c) points. Sign unchanged (registered criterion: moves < 1 point and keeps its sign).
- Falsifier (a cell moving >= 1 point, or 111 moving >= 1 point more than 100) not met: no re-scoring of the 200M F2 numbers in fp32 is needed on this evidence.

Caveat: the emulation is partial (see Method), so it bounds the GDN-boundary rounding, not the whole bf16 path; the CPU fp32 numbers themselves match the GPU/bf16 clean80 to 0.015% (SQ339 devlog), which is the stronger end-to-end evidence. I here (+6.36) is on 300 docs, not the full clean80 (+6.2 in sq263.json).
