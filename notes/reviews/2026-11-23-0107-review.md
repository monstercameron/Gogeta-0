## 2. Critique

### 2a. Architecture (gogeta/model.py)

1. **Init scale is tied to depth, and no C197 arm varies it.** `_init_weights` sets residual-output std to `0.02/sqrt(2*effective_depth)` (model.py:253). `effective_depth` is prelude + coda + r·core at the default loop count (model.py:246-248). So E1 (6 blocks) and the depth arms (E32, E34, E2) run at different residual scales. C199 shows that scale moves all-attention stacks by about 3.5–3.8%. Depth and init are confounded in C197 (unless the E32 config sets `init` explicitly; not checked here).
2. **E2's init uses r=4, while training samples r in U{1..7}** (model.py:267, config default). The init therefore reflects a loop count the training loop mostly does not run.
3. **The loop recipe has never been varied.** E2 combines shared weights, re-injection of e every pass, no loop-boundary norm, sampled r, and BPTT truncated to the last 4 passes (model.py:267). The h-norm grows along a near-constant increment (K6, C39). C6/C7 therefore test this recipe, not "looping" in general. E9 removes only the sharing.
4. **FLOP accounting is not wall-clock efficiency.** SWA runs as a dense boolean mask (model.py:133, 144), so on GPU it costs as much as full attention. `flops_per_token` counts SWA at window size (model.py:313). The "0.94× compute" and matched-FLOP claims describe FLOPs the kernel does not realise. C12 is WRONG FOR OUR CODE and C122 measured the hybrid 2.1× slower than E0. Linear-time efficiency is not measured anywhere.
5. **E1 vs E10 differs in more than the mixer:** init (C199), RoPE layout and eps (v0.4.78 limitation), and params (C95, SQ241). Each is measured on its own. The combined difference is never measured.

## 3. Alternative ideas (speculation throughout; costs are estimates)

**A. Init-controlled depth test for C197.**
- Hypothesis (spec): much of E32's gain over E1 is the depth-scaled init, not unique depth.
- Cheapest test: E1 and E32 at 20M tokens, flat vs gogeta init, 2 seeds, LR 6e-3, clean80 (about 25 GPU-min, one 5090).
- Changes plans: a gap under 2% with flat init retracts "lever" to "init artifact". A gap of 5% or more under both inits keeps depth alive.
- Novelty: SQ200/SQ234 test init on E0, E10 and E1 only. No arm varies init on the deeper stacks.

**B. Length-bucketed sampling for C193.**
- Hypothesis (spec): E1's deficit on documents over 1,200 tokens comes from long documents being rare in training, not from the mixer.
- Cheapest test: E1 and E10 at 20M, uniform vs length-bucketed sampling, 2 seeds; score the SQ99 length bins (about 15 GPU-min).
- Changes plans: if the long-bin deficit disappears under bucketing, C193 is a data effect and C16 cannot be read from length alone. If it persists, the mixer limit is real.
- Novelty: SQ190 (repetition) and SQ237 (length extrapolation) do not change the sampling distribution.

**C. Equal wall-clock replot from existing logs (CPU only).**
- Hypothesis (spec): at equal measured wall-clock, E1 ties or loses to dense E10, and E2 loses by more.
- Cheapest test: plot val loss against logged wall-clock and the SQ220 tok/s for E0, E1, E10, E2 and E9. No training.
- Changes plans: if the hybrid loses at equal time, the efficiency pitch (goal 1) waits for a real windowed kernel (SQ72).
- Novelty: SQ96 runs one new pair; SQ244 measures wall-clock. Neither uses existing logs for all arms, including loops.

**D. Depth on the out-of-corpus length bins.**
- Hypothesis (spec): the 14-block E32 does not show E1's long-document deficit (C193).
- Cheapest test: score the existing E32 checkpoint on the 330-file SQ99 set with the SQ99 scorer (minutes; only if the checkpoint is on disk, not checked).
- Changes plans: if E32 wins the over-1,200 bin, depth rather than the mixer is the long-context lever, and the hybrid pitch shifts.
- Novelty: SQ99 scored E0, E1 and E10 only.

**E. Zero-init residual branches (SkipInit-style).**
- Hypothesis (spec): zero-initialising the residual-output projections removes the all-attention init penalty, so E0 matches E10 and C199's effect disappears.
- Cheapest test: E0 and E1 at 20M, zero-init vs gogeta, 2 seeds, LR 3e-3 and 6e-3 (about 15 GPU-min).
- Changes plans: if E0 closes the gap and E1 is unchanged, the init issue is a residual-scale issue with a standard fix, and the baseline for every comparison changes.
- Novelty: C199 varied only the std. SQ11 (Muon) and SQ26 (gated residual) change other things.
