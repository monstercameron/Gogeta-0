## Triage
- One-claim newcomer walkthrough: already covered twice. SQ274's first manual case is the C209 trail (from the 13:44 review), and the 14:24 triage made it a "start here" path if that trace fails. Not added again.
- Blind completion preference (A2s vs E20, 20 prompts): rejected at 13M. Completions from 15-25M-parameter models are too weak for 20 blind comparisons to separate two models that differ by 1-9% in bits per char. A human-preference measure also needs human raters, and the human's time is not budgeted for labelling. It is kept as a final-model eval idea (E61 scale, next to Q14's pass@1), not a 13M test.
- Question (which outcome is the main target?): efficiency at fixed quality on code, measured as quality per training FLOP and per bit of weight memory (README pitch, goal 1). The quality measure is clean80 bits per char, with out-of-corpus quality as the generalisation check (SQ379/SQ400). Completion usefulness (pass@1, goal 6) is the final-model test, because at 13M it sits at a floor of about 2%. So a newcomer should read every result as "how much quality per FLOP and per byte does this component buy", with clean80 as the scale.
