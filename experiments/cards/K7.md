# K7: LR tuning for E10 and E1

## Question and prediction
- **Question:** every model so far used lr 3e-3, untuned (claim C92). What's each model's best peak LR at a 20M-token proxy?
- **Prediction (committed before running):** `experiments/checks/PREDICTIONS.md`, section "K7".
  - E10's best is 3e-3 or 6e-3; E1's best is 2e-3 or 3e-3.
  - Both are flat near the optimum, and 1e-3 is 3–8% worse.
- **Decision rule:**
  - The lowest final full-val loss wins, and ties go to the smaller LR.
  - If the winner is at an edge, extend the grid one step: 5e-4 below or 1.2e-2 above, then 2.5e-4 / 2.4e-2.

## How to run it
- **Runs:** `bash tools/launch_campaign.sh experiments/launch/campaigns/phase2a-k7-lr.campaign`.
  - 8 launches, each `E10|E1 --seed 0 --lr X --max-tokens 20000000`, through the experiment launcher.
  - Needs the launcher's `--lr` pass-through.
- **Analysis (CPU):** `~/.venvs/gogeta/bin/python experiments/checks/k7_lr_sweep.py [--commit <sha>] [--write-campaign]`.
  - Writes `experiments/checks/results/K7.json`.
  - Then writes either `phase2a-k7-lr-ext.campaign` (grid extension) or `phase2b-replication.campaign`.
- **Compute:** ~43 min GPU, estimated from v0.3.0 throughputs plus ~2 min overhead per run and 60 s gaps.

**Final (v0.4.26):** the extension run at 1.2e-2 gave 1.0656 (+7.4%), so **E1's tuned LR is 6e-3, bracketed**, and **E10's is 3e-3**. The rule wrote `experiments/launch/campaigns/phase2b-replication.campaign` (13 runs × 200M tokens, ~7 GPU-h): E10 ×3 at 3e-3, E1 ×3, E11 ×3, E9 and E2 at 6e-3. **Stage 1 (v0.4.25), 8 runs, 20M tokens, seed 0** (`experiments/checks/results/K7.json`):
- **E10: tuned LR = 3e-3**, bracketed. 2e-3 is +3.54%, 6e-3 is +5.86%, 1e-3 is +20.4%.
- **E1: undecided.** The best is 6e-3 (0.9925), at the top edge of the grid; 3e-3 is +1.47%, 2e-3 +4.46%, 1e-3 +17.1%. Decision rule step 2 extends to 1.2e-2 (one run).
- **Prediction scorecard:**
  - E10 best at 3e-3 or 6e-3: **hit**.
  - E1 best at 2e-3 or 3e-3: **miss** (6e-3 or higher).
  - Best vs second best < 1%: **miss** (E10 3.5%, E1 1.5%); the curves are sharper than predicted.
  - 1e-3 is +3–8% worse: **miss** (+17–20%, much worse).
  - No divergence at 6e-3: **hit**.
  - Neither winner at the low edge: **hit**.
- **Implication:** every v0.3.0 FusionDance result (E1, E2, E3 at 3e-3) probably ran below its best LR, while E0/E10 at 3e-3 is near-optimal, so E1 vs E0 (C5) may *understate* the hybrid's advantage. Ledger C92 → PARTIAL.

## How to reuse this
- The `k7_lr_sweep.py` pattern works for any future sweep (E2, E3, a larger control): filter runs by `cli_overrides`, apply a pre-registered rule, emit the next campaign.
- Don't treat the picked LR as optimal at other horizons or batch sizes.

## Caveats
- Short-horizon proxy: the optimal LR usually falls with longer training.
- Warmup is 49% of a 20M-token run.
- One seed per LR.
- E2 and E3 aren't tuned.
