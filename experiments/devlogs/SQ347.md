# SQ347: copy-deficit probe on the 200M F2 cells

**Status:** done (registered in PREDICTIONS.md section SQ347, committed in v0.5.3 before the run) · **Verdict:** registered prediction NOT met at gaps 256 and 512 (I_copy +3.5 and +3.3 points, 95% CIs include 0) and the falsifier is not met either (the sign is positive at all gaps); at gap 128 I_copy = +8.95 points with CI excluding 0. Inconclusive for "the anti-synergy is a recall deficit" · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq347_copy_deficit.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq347.json`

## Method (as registered)
All five cells at best LR, seeds 0-2, fp32 CPU (GDN via the chunkwise torch kernel, validated against GPU to 0.015%). Sequences: BOS, 64 pre-filler tokens, a 32-token span S, G filler tokens, S again; G in {128, 256, 512}; 200 sequences per gap, the same for every cell and seed (random.Random(0) windows cut from the concatenated token stream of the 2,333 clean80 v1-val docs; the v2 set itself is not local, see SQ339 devlog). y = log of the mean NLL (nats/token) on the second copy's 32 tokens. I_copy = y111 - y100 - y010 - y001 + 2 y000 with the seed-blocked estimator of sq263.py (df 8). Reference I_bpc (sq263.json seed-blocked) = +6.21 points. Also recorded: NLL of the first occurrence of the span (no copy available).

## Reading and caveats
- The GDN-containing cells (100, 111) copy much worse than the dense cells at gap 256: +32% and +37% NLL vs dense (gap 128: +5% / +9%; gap 512: +7% / +6%, where all cells have lost most of the copy gain). That is a recall deficit of the GDN hybrid on this probe, as expected from SWA windows of 256 plus a recurrent state; the interaction I_copy, however, mixes this with the other cells and is imprecise at n = 3 (CI half-width about 6.5 points). Loops (010) and ternary (001) alone are within about 5% of dense at every gap.
- I_copy is positive in all three gaps and in 8 of 9 seed-gap cells, but the registered criterion (CI excludes 0 at 256 and 512) is not met. Neither the claim "recall deficit explains the anti-synergy" nor its refutation (|I_copy| < 1 point or negative at both gaps) is supported by this run.
- Synthetic spans are natural 32-token code windows (often starting mid-identifier), not identifiers; 200 sequences x 32 tokens per cell and gap. Descriptive beyond the registered test.
