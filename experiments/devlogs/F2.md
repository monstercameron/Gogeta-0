  - **Cell means (clean80 bpc):**

    | Cell | Ingredients | bpc |

- **Read:** the ingredients' penalties overlap. The full fusion is about 3.5% better than the additive prediction, but still about 11% worse than dense at 20M. GDN alone helps (−7.3%); loops (+13%) and ternary (+9.8%) hurt at this horizon.
- **Registered prediction was wrong:** it said I = +0.5% ± 1.5% and that the full fusion would be the worst cell; loops-only is the worst.
- **Caveats:**
  - **20M horizon only.** SQ263 (200M, the five cells, plus 2e-3 twins) is the registered check.
  - **Blinding:** stage-1 per-run scores were visible in the monitor, and 67 score files were committed in v0.4.88. The estimand, model and N rule were fixed beforehand, and no contrast was computed before this run.
  - **Allocation:** E20's 37 extra seeds (37–73) carry no information for the blocked contrast; they only feed the unblocked sensitivity analysis.
- **Ledger:** C201, VERIFIED with those limitations.

- **Follow-ups (v0.4.104, descriptive, not registered):**
  - **Scale:** the I and per-cell figures above are log-points (100 x difference in log bpc). As a ratio, I = +6.21 log-points is +6.4%.
  - **Per seed (SQ336):** I = +8.83%, +3.94% and +5.86% for seeds 0, 1 and 2. Leaving any one seed out gives +4.90% to +7.35%, so no single seed carries the sign.
  - **Along training (SQ337;** `experiments/checks/sq337_sq338.py`; in-training eval loss, not clean80**):** I = -4.0% at step 250. It crosses 0 near step 3,250 (~107M tokens) and climbs steadily to +4.5% at the last eval. The 20M synergy and the 200M anti-synergy are one trajectory read at two points, and it is still rising at 200M.
  - **Equal wall-clock (SQ338):** the GDN cell takes 1.62x the dense wall time on a 5090, the full fusion 1.55x. At the dense cell's wall-clock, GDN is +8.2% worse than dense (−3.4% at equal steps) and the full fusion +31.7%. These are truncated-cosine reads, pessimistic against a schedule that ends at that budget.
  - **Unique params:** looping halves the unique parameters at matched block applications (12.3M and 12.6M for cells 010 and 111, vs 27.9M and 29.2M for 000 and 100). So the loops factor is also a unique-params factor, consistent with C197.
