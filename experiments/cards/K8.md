# K8: External sanity reference in bits per character

## Question and prediction
- **Question:** are our ~0.23 bits/char on held-out Python plausible for this corpus, or a sign of a bug or train/val leakage (claim C94)?
- **Prediction (committed before running):** `experiments/checks/PREDICTIONS.md`, section "K8".
  - SmolLM2-135M at 0.28–0.45 bits/char; ours at 0.21–0.26.
  - Our doc-level bits/char matches the training-time value within 5%.
- **Pass criteria:**
  - K8a: ours ≥ 0.5 × SmolLM2's bits/char; otherwise check for leakage.
  - K8b: doc-level within 5% of `val_bits_per_char`.

## How to run it
- **When:** after Phase 2b. It's a GPU check: take the slot only when `bash experiments/launch/status.sh` shows it free with no GPU process.
- **Command:**
  `~/.venvs/gogeta/bin/python experiments/checks/k8_external_reference.py --external --checkpoint ~/fusiondance-data/checkpoints/<E10 tuned s0>/model.pt --checkpoint ~/fusiondance-data/checkpoints/<E1 tuned s0>/model.pt`
- `--plan` prints what would be loaded.
- **The script never downloads:**
  - it loads with `cache_dir=~/fusiondance-data/hf-cache` and `local_files_only=True`;
  - the revision is pinned to `93efa2f097d58c2a74874c7e644dbc9b0cee75a2`;
  - it refuses to run if `notes/smollm2-reference.md` names a different revision.
- **Compute:** ~5 min GPU, estimated (≈1.1M SmolLM2 tokens plus 2 × 1.06M of ours).

## How to reuse this
- `score()` / `score_checkpoint()` give tokenizer-independent bits/char for any of our checkpoints (FusionDance or hf_llama). Use them to compare models with different tokenizers.
- Not a control: SmolLM2 differs in size, data and tokenizer.

## Caveats
- SmolLM2-135M is ~10× our size and trained on ~2T tokens of different data.
- Contamination with our corpus is possible and unchecked.
- It's a sanity ceiling, not a control.
