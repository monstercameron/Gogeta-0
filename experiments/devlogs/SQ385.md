# SQ385: position-resolved and length-resolved assembly costs

**Status:** done for the position and length bins (exploratory, from SQ239's per-doc re-score); the distractor-sensitivity arm was NOT run · **Verdict:** the costs are concentrated early in a document and reverse late: the loop cost (A2f vs E24) is +2.5% in tokens 1-128 and +2.0% in 129-512, then -3.1% [-5.0, -1.1] in 513-1024; the ternary cost (A2s vs A2f) is +7.0% and +6.7% in the first two bins and +0.2% at 513-1024, while the assembly gain grows with position (-11.6% in 1-128, -25.6% at 513-1024, -30.9% beyond 1024). The first-128-token cost is not hidden by the aggregate: it is 1.1-1.6x the cost on the rest · **Links:** `experiments/checks/sq239_analysis.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq385.json`

## Method
Per-token NLL of the 30 checkpoints binned by absolute token position in the doc (1-128, 129-512, 513-1024, 1025+; nats/token, effect = exp(difference) - 1) on the clean80 docs, and per-doc bits/char by document length bin (<=128, 129-256, 257-512, 513-1024 tokens; too few docs above 1,024 for a bin); paired-by-seed, t-CI.

## Reading and caveats
- Beyond about 512 tokens the loops and ternary stop costing and the assembly gain is largest; the sign change across position bins is real for the loop cost (the CI excludes 0 at 513-1024) and calls for a position/length qualifier on the loop-cost headline. The costs being paid mostly in the first 512 tokens suggests that SQ383's coda-attention lever (long-range copy) is not what generates them. The 1025+ bin rests on very few tokens (wide CIs).
- Position and overlap are confounded (long docs are the synthetic-snippet ones with a different overlap profile); these are paired contrasts, not causal. The distractor-sensitivity extension (prepend unrelated in-repo code before held-out targets) was not run: no result for it.
