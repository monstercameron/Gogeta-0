# SQ377: block duplication at inference on unlooped checkpoints (RYS-style)

**Status:** done (descriptive, not registered) · **Verdict:** no. Re-applying any single block or adjacent block pair of the unlooped 200M GDN hybrid E24 or the 14M hybrid E1 makes the model worse in every variant and every seed: E24's best variant (block 4 applied twice) is +1.27% bits/char (95% CI +0.25 to +2.28), E1's best is +11.5%. Extra inference-time depth from duplication is not free quality for these models · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq377_block_duplication.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq377.json`

## Method
E24 (14 blocks: swa gdn gdn swa gdn gdn swa gdn gdn swa gdn gdn swa attn; best LR 3e-3, seeds 0-2) and E1 (6-block hybrid, 200M tokens on v1, lr 6e-3, seeds 0-2). In turn each block j is applied twice in a row (order ... j, j, j+1 ...; "dup_j") and each adjacent pair twice ("pair_j_j+1": ... j, j+1, j, j+1 ...); embeddings, final norm and head unchanged, no training. Metric: aggregate bits/char on the first 200 docs of the 300-doc clean80 subset (fp32 CPU); effect = 100 x (bpc_variant / bpc_base - 1) per seed, mean and 95% t-CI (df 2). E32 (listed in the SQ377 row) was not scored: no 200M checkpoint of it is among the local ones.

## Result (n = 3 seeds per arm)
- E24 (base 0.2601 / 0.2618 / 0.2612): 0 of 27 variants beat the base. Single-block duplications, mean effect: block 1 (+1.42%), 2 (+2.55%), 3 (+3.59%), 4 (+1.27%, the best), 5 (+5.0%), 6 (+5.5%), 7 (+5.3%), then +6.3 to +7.4% for blocks 8-12 and +10.8% for the final attention block 14; pairs from +2.8% (blocks 1-2) to +15.4% (blocks 13-14).
- E1 (base 0.2867 / 0.2877 / 0.2878): 0 of 11 variants beat the base: best +11.5% (block 2 duplicated), +11.9% (block 4), +12.8% (block 5), block 1 +46.7%, pairs +13.5% to +22.5%.
(Block numbers are 1-based in this text; the JSON keys `dup_j` are 0-based.)

## Reading and caveats
- The mildest damage is at the earliest blocks of E24 and at one mid block (+1.3%), in the region RYS-style work reports as repeatable in large LMs; at 200M parameters (and 14M for E1) no block is a "reasoning circuit" that tolerates repetition, and the deficit is larger for the smaller model. Any loop benefit in these models therefore has to be trained for (SQ341's looped cells are best at their trained r).
- Only an unscaled re-application was tried (no per-copy gain, no LayerScale/gate interpolation between the original and repeated residual). 200 docs; descriptive, n = 3 seeds.
