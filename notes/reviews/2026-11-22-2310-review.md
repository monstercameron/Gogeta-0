## 2. Critique

**Wave 5 (factorial F2, N = 246)**

1. **The sizing rests on 10 degrees of freedom.** The pooled sigma 0.009 is the RMS of five per-cell sds, each from 3 seeds (computed from the JSON: E20 0.64%, E21 1.05%, E22 1.17%, E24 0.18%, E27 1.08%). A 10-df pooled sd has a rough 95% range of x0.6 to x1.4 (computed, chi-square). N scales with sd², so the registered N could be off by about 2x either way (computed range about 80 to 500). "The pilot decides" holds only if that range is priced in.
2. **The noise is LR-shaped.** The three 6e-3 cells have sd near 1.1%; the 3e-3 cells have 0.2 to 0.6%. Seed-0 clean80 for E22 is 0.793 at 1.2e-2, 0.535 at 3e-3 and 0.525 at 6e-3 (computed from JSON): the 20M argmin sits near a stability edge (spec: that is the likely source of the high sd).
3. **The LR does not transfer across horizon, or equally across architectures.** At 200M, E10 at 6e-3 is 3.0% worse than at 2e-3 (H2, one seed, computed). E1 ties at 3e-3 and 6e-3 (H2). A 200M factorial run at the F0 LRs would confound interaction with LR-horizon mismatch, and the design does not separate them.
4. **The regime is wrong for the thesis.** The headline claims are at 200M. At 20M the cell means already differ by about 20%: E24 (3e-3) 0.425 against E27 (6e-3) 0.511, three seeds each (computed). A 1% interaction is being tested where main effects are 10 to 30%. If E24 is a single ingredient and E27 the full stack, the 20M data already say the full stack is worse than a part, and 231 new runs add little to the 20M decision.
5. **The metric cannot decide "beats the sum of its parts" in the efficiency sense.** The README headline is quality per FLOP and per bit. The factorial compares cells at matched tokens, but loop cells use about 2x the FLOPs (E2) and ternary cells use fewer bits. The additive reference must be defined per FLOP and per bit, or a "stacks" verdict is about quality alone.
6. **Allocation is not the weak point.** For the additive-deviation contrast D = Y111 − Y100 − Y010 − Y001 + 2·Y000 (my reading of the 5-cell plan, unverified), the variance-optimal allocation is n proportional to |coefficient|, which gives E20 its 2:1 share (computed). The gains lie in sd and regime, not allocation.
7. **Possible variance cut, unexamined.** Seed residuals correlate across cells (computed from the 15 F0/F1 runs, n = 3, indicative only): E20–E21 0.96, E21–E27 0.89, E24–E27 0.76, E20–E27 0.72, but E22–E24 −0.96. If a seed fixes the data order identically across architectures, a seed-blocked contrast removes shared variance. If the registered analysis treats cells as independent, N is too large.

**Wave 4 (SQ243 two-size, SQ241 param-matched)**

8. **Power is smaller than the effect.** The paired E1−E10 sd at d = 384 is about 0.45% (Phase 2b, computed). At 4 seeds the MDE is about 0.9% (SQ253). The expected gap is about 0.2%. So S1 and S2 ("within ±1%") will return "tie" whatever the truth, and S3 (only gaps beyond 0.9% count) is almost unreachable. Resolving 0.3% at each end needs about 18 seeds per arm (computed: n ≈ (2.8 × 0.45 / 0.3)²). Either call the design descriptive or narrow the scaling claim.
9. **Fixed LRs across widths confound width with LR mis-tuning** (E1 6e-3, E10 3e-3 at every d). The optimum LR usually moves with width (spec; not tested here). Given item 3, a d = 512 gap may reflect one arm's LR fragility rather than architecture.
10. **"Within ±1%" is an equivalence claim with no registered TOST margin.** C196's ±1% margin is already flagged (22:30 review, critique 6). Register a margin with a practical justification before the runs.
11. **SQ241 P2 ("no more than 0.5% worse than full E1") is below the 4-seed MDE**, so it cannot reject a 0.5% loss. P1 compares a 4-seed arm with a 7-seed mean. Use matched n or a paired test.

**Method, general**

12. Only the d = 384, 200M comparison has n of at least 7. Everything else is at most 4 seeds at 20M, so the thesis regime is the least tested.
13. Population choice is unresolved: clean80 keeps about 48% of algorithmic-corpus docs but about 77% of synthetic ones (SQ225). SQ250 and SQ252 are registered but unrun and should precede any "sum of parts" verdict.

**A. Seed-blocked factorial contrast on existing runs** (new)
- Hypothesis (spec): same-seed runs share data order, so seed residuals correlate across cells, and a seed-blocked D has at most 60% of the independent-cell SE.
- Cheapest test: CPU only, on the 15 F0/F1 runs in fleet_2026-11-22.json; compute D with and without seed blocking. Minutes.
- Changes plans: if the blocked SE is at most 60% of the unblocked, re-size F2 by a new registration before dispatch. If not, drop it.
- Novelty: SQ94 and SQ224 are leave-one-seed-out on E1/E10 only. Nothing on blocked factorial contrasts.

**B. Common-random-numbers check** (new; gates A)
- Hypothesis (spec): for a given seed, the first 1,000 batches are identical across E10, E1, E2 and the factorial cells.
- Cheapest test: CPU, hash the first batch token ids per seed for two configs. Seconds.
- Changes plans: identical means A is valid; different means A is void and the independent-cell sizing stands.
- Novelty: SQ221 covers data-build determinism, not batch-order determinism.

**C. In-regime 200M factorial gate** (new as a gate; the F-series is 20M only)
- Hypothesis (spec): at 200M the cell sd is about 0.3% (E1/E10 proxy) and the 20M ranking (E24 best, E27 worst) survives.
- Cheapest test: 5 cells × 3 seeds at 200M at the F0 LRs, plus 2e-3 twins for the 6e-3 cells: 15 to 21 runs at about 4.5 min each (computed from SQ220 throughput), about 1.1 to 1.6 GPU-h.
- Changes plans: a ranking flip, or a 6e-3 cell losing more than 2% to its 2e-3 twin, means 20M is not a proxy and F2 moves to 200M. If the ranking holds with sd at most 0.4%, n = 4 per cell at 200M gives about ±0.7% on D (computed) for about 1.8 GPU-h.

**D. Per-FLOP and per-bit reading of the factorial contrast** (new reformulation; nearest is SQ32/SQ33)
- Hypothesis (spec): the sign of D flips between matched-token and matched-FLOP readings, because loop cells spend about 2x the FLOPs.
- Cheapest test: CPU re-analysis of existing A3 and F cells using flops_per_token (gogeta/model.py:289) and bits per weight. No GPU.
- Changes plans: a sign flip means the registered metric cannot answer the thesis, and the metric must change before F2 is spent.

**E. Iso-bytes ternary at 20M** (new)
- Hypothesis (spec): ternary E1 at 2x width (4x params, about 0.4x E10's weight bytes at 16-bit, computed) matches E10 on clean80 at 20M.
- Cheapest test: one 20M run at d = 768 (about 4x FLOPs, a few minutes on a 5090, estimate) against E10 at d = 384, seed 0, both on clean80.
- Changes plans: a match or win supports an iso-memory bits claim. A loss leaves ternary's case resting on speed, which is currently negative (C14).
- Novelty: C9 and C13 are computed bytes; SQ1 is the embedding table. No iso-bytes run exists.

**F. LR fragility of the high-sd cells** (adjacent to SQ12 and SQ217; new as a factorial variance test)
