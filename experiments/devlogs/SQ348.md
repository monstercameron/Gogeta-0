# SQ348: per-pass logit lens on the 200M F2 loop cells

**Status:** done (descriptive, not registered; relative comparisons only) · **Verdict:** the loop penalty is not only a readout effect: reading the hidden state after pass k directly (no coda) improves until k ~ 7-8 then worsens, and the full-fusion cell is far better than the dense loop cell at k = 1 (0.422 vs 0.497 bpc), i.e. the gap to dense does not appear at the entry · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq341_sq348_loops.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq348.json`

## Method
Same pass as SQ341 (cells 010 and 111, best LR, seeds 0-2, 300 clean80 docs, fp32 CPU). For each pass k = 1..12 two readouts of h_k: with_coda = head(norm_out(coda(h_k))) (the real model at loops = k) and no_coda = head(norm_out(h_k)), the logit lens that bypasses the coda. The head and norm_out were trained on coda outputs, so no_coda bpc is not the model's loss: use it only to compare across k and cells.

## Result (mean bpc over 3 seeds)
| k | 010 no_coda | 010 with_coda | 111 no_coda | 111 with_coda |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.4966 | 0.3102 | 0.4221 | 0.3634 |
| 2 | 0.4302 | 0.2909 | 0.3868 | 0.3427 |
| 4 | 0.3998 | 0.2865 | 0.3740 | 0.3387 |
| 6 | 0.3923 | 0.2885 | 0.3732 | 0.3414 |
| 8 | 0.3908 | 0.2923 | 0.3748 | 0.3454 |
| 12 | 0.3937 | 0.3027 | 0.3794 | 0.3534 |
Minimum of no_coda: k = 8 for 010 (0.3908), k = 5-6 for 111 (0.3731). The coda is worth 0.09 bpc (23%) in 010 and 0.035 (9%) in 111 at k = 4.

## Reading and caveats
- Entry pass: 111 at k = 1 is much better than 010 without the coda (0.422 vs 0.497) but worse with it (0.363 vs 0.310); so the fused core's single pass carries more usable information for the head directly, while the dense cell relies on its coda. The relative cost of the fusion (vs 010, with coda) is therefore not set at k = 1: it is +17% at k = 1 and +18% at k = 4 (0.3634/0.3102, 0.3387/0.2865).
- Over passes the lens curves flatten after k ~ 4-6 while with_coda also degrades past 4: the extra passes shift the state away from what the coda was trained to read.
- Lens numbers depend on a head trained for a different input; do not read absolute levels. 300 docs, n = 3 seeds, descriptive.
