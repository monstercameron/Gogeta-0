## 2. Critique

### 2a. Architecture (gogeta/model.py)

1. **Recurrence precision is untested at LM level (code-read).** The gate math is fp32, but q, k and v are cast to bf16 and the kernel runs in bf16 on CUDA (model.py:209-217). Every GDN-only lever (C197, C201, C202, E11) rests on this. The only check is a one-step error test on random inputs (cited under C2 in the 0308 review). No loss is scored in fp32 on real data. SQ222 was promoted in v0.4.102, but as a whole-model check, not a per-cell one.
2. **"Ternary" is two ingredients (code-read).** BitLinear (model.py:88-99) applies absmean ternary weights and per-token int8 absmax activations, both with STE, and `inject` uses it too when `ternary=True`. The F2 ternary penalty (+15.8%) is therefore W1.58 plus A8. Nothing on disk separates them. SQ192 proposed the split; I found no record that it ran. The claim that ternary weights are the expensive ingredient is not yet supported.
3. **Every F2 effect is measured against one init (model.py:61-64, 274-284; v0.4.99).** F2 uses the default gogeta init. SQ200 found that this init costs 6-block attention about 3.5%, and SQ308 found that it helps the 14-block hybrid about 7%. The GDN, loop and ternary effects are therefore measured against a dense baseline whose handicap depends on depth. SQ326 (the init-matched factorial) was accepted in v0.4.99. I found no result for it.
4. **Possible parameter confound in the loop factor (to check).** The design fixes block applications (14, FLOPs-matched). An unshared dense cell has more unique blocks than a looped cell. C197 names unique parameters as the lever, so "loops cost 10.6%" may partly be "fewer unique blocks". Check `param_counts()` per cell; I could not read the configs.
5. **Init RNG coupling (refines 0308 item 2).** `_init_weights` (model.py:274-284) draws from the global RNG in module order, after the constructors have already drawn. Arms with different shapes therefore get different init streams from the same seed. Seeds alone cannot fix this across architectures. The right control is per-tensor generators keyed by parameter name, so shared tensors share their init (this refines SQ332).

### 2b. Method

6. **The 200M I is a sum-of-effects deviation, not an ingredient pair.** I = y111 - y100 - y010 - y001 + 2·y000 on the log scale equals (y111 - y000) minus the sum of the three single-ingredient effects (computed). It cannot say which pair carries the reversal. It is +6.2% on the log scale and about +8.4% on the ratio scale (computed from the v0.4.103 table). The README quotes only the log value.
7. **The 200M LR is unclear.** The table header says "each cell's best LR", but the H2' text says "all three six-e-3 cells", which implies some cells ran at other LRs. If so, I mixes LR choice with the ingredients. H2' also checks only 2e-3, one side of 6e-3; no 1.2e-2 result at 200M is reported.
8. **Wall-clock is missing from the headline.** The GDN cell costs about 1.6x the wall time at 20M (v0.4.98). The README's headline metric is quality per FLOP, and "GDN is the only ingredient that helps" does not say so.
9. **The robustness checks are weak at n=3.** Leave-one-seed-out (SQ336) leaves 2 seeds per cell, so it can detect little. A CI that already excludes 0 with df 8 gains little from "no sign flip". A per-seed table or a seed bootstrap would say more.
10. **Wording against status (AGENTS §0).** The README states "the ingredients' penalties compound" as fact while C202 is PARTIAL. "GDN alone is 4% better than dense" carries no caveat for item 3. The status paragraph says E1 is "0.18% worse" where the CI spans zero.

## 3. Alternative ideas

Each idea gives a hypothesis (spec), the cheapest test, what would change our plans, and novelty. CPU or GPU is marked.

**A1. Split the ternary factor (CPU).** Hypothesis (spec): most of the +15.8% is int8 activation rounding, not ternary weights. Test: post-hoc eval-time fake quantisation on the existing 200M E1 and cell-000/111 checkpoints, weight-only, activation-only and both, scored on clean80 over the 330 SQ99 docs. About 10 CPU-min, no training. Changes plans: if activation rounding explains over half, the ternary track should start with int8 activations. Novelty: SQ192 is a training arm (not run) and SQ21 is post-hoc on E2 weights only; the activation-only arm is new.

**A2. Precision compounding in the GDN-loop cell (CPU).** Hypothesis (spec): bf16 recurrence error is repeated on every loop pass, so cell 111 carries extra precision penalty and part of the reversal is numeric. Test: score cells 111, 000 and 100 (3 seeds each) on the 330 SQ99 docs with the plain-PyTorch GDN reference in fp32 (export_trace.py, C130). About 20 CPU-min. Changes plans: if I moves by more than 1 point, every 200M F2 score is re-run in fp32 before C202 moves. Novelty: SQ222 covers E1 vs E10 only; the cell-level, loop-compounding test is new.

**A3. Loop-count curve per F2 cell (CPU).** Hypothesis (spec): the fused cell's loss minimum sits at a different r than dense, so the +10.6% loop penalty depends on the chosen r. Test: evaluate r = 1..12 on the 12 existing 200M F2 checkpoints over 330 docs. Inference only, about 40 CPU-min across slots. Changes plans: if 111 keeps improving past r = 4 while 010 peaks there, "loops cost" is a property of r, not of looping. Novelty: the C7 and SQ14 curves exist for E2 only.

**A4. Equal-wall-clock re-read of F2 at 200M (CPU, computed).** Hypothesis (spec): at equal wall time the GDN cell's -4.0% shrinks toward zero, since it runs about 1.6x slower. Test: compare clean80 at equal wall time from the per-step logs already on disk (check that they record time). Minutes, no run. Changes plans: if GDN's lead disappears, the lever claim becomes FLOP-specific and the README says so. Novelty: SQ96 does equal-time for E10/E1 on GPU; the F2 log version is new and free.

**A5. Per-cell LR at 20M (GPU).** Hypothesis (spec): the 20M synergy and the 200M reversal are both LR artifacts if cells have different optima. Test: 5 cells x {3e-3, 6e-3, 1.2e-2} x 2 seeds at 20M, 30 runs at about 0.6-0.9 min each (v0.4.98), roughly 25 GPU-min. This needs the GPU budget, so it is the human's call. Changes plans: if I stays near -3% with per-cell optima, the 20M sign is robust to LR; if it moves to zero, C201's 20M result is LR-dependent. Novelty: SQ328 is a descriptive LR-column check with n=1, so this overlaps; per-cell optima with 2 seeds are new.

**A6. Decay-only mixer inside the fused cell (GPU).** Hypothesis (spec): the anti-synergy needs the delta-rule write, because loops re-write the state; a decay-only gated linear mixer (same heads and gate) in cell 111 removes most of it. Test: 20M, 2 seeds, cell 111 with GDN vs decay-only, plus cell 000 for reference: 6 runs, about 6 GPU-min. Changes plans: if the penalty goes away, the reversal is about the write rule, and the state-tracking story must be about the delta rule. Novelty: the delta-rule ablation SQ235 exists; the fused-cell version is new.

**A7. Loop placement by block kind (GPU).** Hypothesis (spec): in a GDN hybrid, looping only the attention/SWA block keeps most of the loop gain while avoiding the GDN interaction. Test: E2-style layout at 20M, 2 seeds, arms loop-SWA-only, loop-GDN-only and full loop: 6 runs, about 6 GPU-min. Changes plans: if loop-SWA-only matches the full loop, the fused design should loop only the local block. Novelty: MixerLoop (SQ32) loops the mixer and runs the MLP once, the reverse of this; placement by block kind is not queued.

**A8. Identifier-renaming probe (CPU).** Hypothesis (spec): E1's in-corpus lead partly rests on memorised identifier strings, so consistent renaming of local names in the 330 SQ99 docs shrinks E1's gap to E10. Test: score E1 and E10 (2 seeds) on original vs renamed docs. The token-level renamer must round-trip exactly and needs its own tests. About 15 CPU-min. Changes plans: a shrink of more than half makes the in-corpus lead lexical, which supports C192 and C193 as written. Novelty: SQ194 changes formatting and SQ90/SQ334 probe bindings, but none uses a renaming control.
