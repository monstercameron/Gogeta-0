# SQ379: do the A2 results hold out of corpus?

**Status:** done (descriptive, not registered) · **Verdict:** yes for the assembly gain, with attenuation: on the SQ99 out-of-corpus set A2s is 11.3% better than the full fusion E27 (clean80: 16.7%), A2f is 1.7% WORSE than dense E20 (clean80: 1.1% better) and the ternary penalty A2s vs A2f vanishes (+0.2%, CI includes 0; clean80: +8-10%). All three comparisons are less favourable to dense-vs-fusion differences than in-corpus text is · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq379_a2_out_of_corpus.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq379.json`; set from `sq_99.json`

## Method
200M checkpoints, best LRs (A2s, A2f, E27 at 6e-3; E24, E20 at 3e-3), paired by seed 2, 3, 4 (A2s/A2f/E27/E20 have all three; E24 only seeds 2 and 4 exist on the pulls). Scored on the SQ99 out-of-corpus set: 330 local Python library files (K9-scanner overlap < 20% with train; sha256 re-verified here, none dropped) by the CPU scorer (fp32, GDN via the chunkwise torch kernel, K8 windowing, window 1024). Effect = mean over paired seeds of 100 x (bpc_arm / bpc_base - 1) on the aggregate bits/char; 95% CI by a package-clustered bootstrap (31 packages as clusters, B = 2000, the resampled doc set shared by all arms and seeds, percentile CI). The CI covers document/package sampling only, not seed variation (n = 2-3 seeds; per-seed effects are listed). In-corpus orientation: the same ratios on the 300-doc clean80 subset (SQ339 subset), per seed, no CI.

## Reading and caveats
- The assembly claim "A2s repairs most of the fusion penalty" survives out of corpus (-11.3% vs -16.7%, seeds all within 0.3 points), so it is not an in-corpus memorisation artefact. "Parity with dense" does not: A2f is +1.7% vs dense out of corpus (it was -1.1% in-corpus on the 300-doc subset), and E27 is +14.8% vs dense instead of +28.7%.
- The ternary cost (A2s vs A2f) is +8-10% in-corpus but about 0 out of corpus (per-seed signs mixed). Out-of-corpus loss levels are 1.65-1.9 bits/char, 6-7x the in-corpus ones, so relative differences compress: all effects shrink toward 0 here, which is partly a scale effect; a model-quality gap cannot be read from compressed ratios.
- The package-clustered CI excludes 0 for most rows but is conditional on the 3 seeds that exist; seed-to-seed spread (sd 1-2 points for A2f vs E20, A2s vs A2f) is larger than the cluster CI half-widths there. E24 has 2 seeds only. Descriptive, not registered.
