## Triage
- Pass-to-pass CKA (A2f vs E24): merged into SQ353, which already compares pass-to-pass cosine and relative norm change for A2s vs A2f. CKA between pass outputs, against depth-matched blocks of E24, is added as a third readout.
- Eval-time reordering of the core blocks: rejected. Permuting a trained model's block order without retraining measures how far the weights fit their trained order. It would hurt for any architecture and says little about whether mixer composition matters. A useful version retrains each order, which is GPU work with low priority next to D1-D4.
- Question (what would show that looped passes do distinct useful transformations, beyond final loss?): three observations, from cheapest up.
  1. The per-pass readout improves monotonically up to the trained r (SQ348 already shows this for the 200M loop cells: r = 1 is about 8% worse than r = 4).
  2. Successive passes are not near-idempotent (SQ353: pass-to-pass CKA well below 1, comparable to the change between distinct unshared blocks of E24).
  3. The decisive one: at matched unique parameters, the looped core beats the same core applied once (SQ350, the parameter-matched loop control) by more than its extra FLOPs buy in an unshared stack (C197's per-FLOP rate).
  (1) and (2) only show the passes are not redundant. (3) shows they are worth their compute.
