# E1: Linear-time mixers (hybrid)

## Question and prediction
- **Question:** can Gated DeltaNet + sliding-window layers with ONE full-attention layer match the dense baseline?
- **Prediction:** registry E1.prediction (committed `0dc7491`). Within ±2% of E0, probably better → direction hit, size missed (−3.6%).

## Results
−3.6% val loss vs E0 with 4% more params and 6% fewer FLOPs; ahead of E0 at all 25 periodic evals; reached E0's final quality at ~169M tokens (84% of budget). Samples were *less* coherent than E0's on some prompts (loss ≠ code quality). Curve: `experiments/curves/E1.json`.
