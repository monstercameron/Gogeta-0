### 2026-11-23 · Change
- Moved from `evals/sandbox.py`: `HIDE` (tmpfs over /mnt, /tmp, /dev/shm, /run, /var/tmp), the prelude (mount, exit 97 on failure, nested `unshare -U -m --map-user=65534`), the harness-by-`-c` invocation, exit-97 handling. - `evals/sandbox.py` now calls `rewards.run_tests(network="require_off", fs_isolation="require")` and keeps its self-test and gate; it re-exports `HIDE`/`_PRELUDE` for compatibility. `run_suite.py` untouched.
