## What it will test, in plain English

> **Thinking silently vs thinking out loud.** Chatbots often "reason" by writing out their thinking step by step. That works, but every extra word costs memory and time. A looped model can do extra thinking *internally*, with no extra words.
>
> This experiment gives both approaches the same task and the same compute budget: which one gets more right?
