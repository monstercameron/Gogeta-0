# SQ414: do 20M-token screens predict 200M outcomes?

**Status:** done (descriptive, not registered; no GPU) · **Verdict:** on the arms we have, mostly yes for ordering, no for magnitude. Across 11 arms the in-training val loss at the 20M-token point of the 200M runs ranks the arms like the 200M end (Spearman 0.84, Kendall 0.67; 9 of 55 pairwise orderings, 16%, flip); B1's directly annealed 20M runs (4 arms) reproduce the 200M ordering exactly (6 of 6 pairs, Spearman 1.0). Every named contrast keeps its sign, but most grow 1.5-2.5x by 200M, so a 20M screen understates effect sizes. Neither stated trigger (correlation < 0.5 or > 25% of orderings flipped) is met · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq414_screen_predicts.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq414.json`

## Method
(a) In-training eval curves (launcher logs, `eval step N: val/loss`, 128 val sequences of code-py-v2, every 250 steps) of the 200M runs on the fleet pulls, best LR only, mean over the seeds found: A2s 6, A2f 6, A3m 3, A3mx 3, A3x 3, A3y 3, E20 8, E24 8, E27 5, E21 3, E22 3 seeds. Value at the step nearest 20M tokens (610, interpolated between steps 500 and 750) vs the end (step 6103). (b) B1's direct 20M-token runs (`b1_runs.json`, schedule annealed over 20M tokens; core_lr_mult 1 arms with a 200M counterpart: A2f 2 seeds, A2s 3, A3m 3, E24 2): final val loss and clean80 vs the 200M end. Rank correlations over arms and the share of pairwise orderings that flip. Caveat: the 20M point of a 200M run sits at about peak LR (cosine over 200M tokens), a different state from a run annealed over 20M.

## Result
- In-run 20M point vs 200M end, 11 arms: Spearman 0.836, Kendall tau 0.673, flipped pairs 9 of 55 (16.4%): A2f-A3mx, A2f-A3x, A2s-E20, A3m-A3y, A3m-E20, A3x-E20, A3y-E20, E21-E27, E22-E27. The flips involve arms within about 2% of each other at 200M (dense E20 vs the assembled variants) and the F2 loop cells.
- B1 direct 20M (val loss, and clean80) vs 200M end, 4 arms (A2f, A2s, A3m, E24): Spearman 1.0, 0 of 6 pairs flipped; the in-run 20M point also orders these 4 arms correctly.
- Named contrasts (% difference in val loss; in-run 20M point / B1 20M val (clean80) / 200M end): A3m vs A2s -0.88 / -0.88 (-1.01) / -2.23; A2f vs E24 +1.89 / +0.19 (+1.80) / +2.22; A2s vs A2f +2.15 / +2.26 (+3.80) / +6.50; A2s vs E27 -8.33 / n.a. / -12.52; A3mx vs A2s -2.71 / n.a. / -5.29; E27 vs E20 +8.10 / n.a. / +20.51; E24 vs E20 -4.78 / n.a. / -3.16; A2f vs E20 -2.98 / n.a. / -1.01. All eight keep their sign at 200M.

## Reading and caveats
- A 20M screen did well at ordering here (16% flips, none among the 4 B1 arms) but understated the ternary and fusion costs (A2s vs A2f 2.2 -> 6.5; E27 vs E20 8.1 -> 20.5) and overstated the dense-vs-hybrid differences (E24 vs E20 -4.8 -> -3.2; A2f vs E20 -3.0 -> -1.0): the sign is a reasonable screen, the size is not. The known counterexample, the F2 interaction (-3.5% at 20M vs +6.2% at 200M, C201 -> C202), is an interaction (a contrast of contrasts) that is not among the pairwise arm contrasts here.
- In-training val loss on 128 sequences, not clean80; arm means over unequal seed counts (2-8), no CIs; B1 has only 4 arms with 200M counterparts, so 6 pairs: "no flips" at that n says little. The row's decision (screens may only reject candidates) is not triggered by these numbers, but the magnitude understatement makes a screen unfit for ranking candidates closer than about 2-3%.
