## 3. Alternative ideas (speculation unless quoted)

**A. Fit the endpoint of the C209 gap from existing curves (zero GPU).**
- Hypothesis (spec): the 200M gap is still widening, so the 16.7% is a lower bound at that horizon.
- Cheapest test: CPU, minutes. Fit each arm's in-training eval curves (A2s, E27; seeds 2-4) to a + b·N^(-c), and read the fitted gap at the asymptote and at 200M.
- Would change plans: if the fitted asymptote differs from -16.7% by more than the CI half-width (1.2 points; computed from the per-seed values -17.05, -16.12, -16.82: mean -16.66, sd 0.48, se 0.28, t 4.303), C209 needs a horizon qualifier and SQ380 (600M) moves up.
- Novelty: SQ395 (token ratio) and SQ337 (interaction along training) are the closest. Neither fits an asymptote.

**B. Train-side duplicate reweighting at matched tokens.**
- Hypothesis (spec): the assembly gain and the loop cost on clean80 are partly driven by near-duplicate training documents. Down-weighting duplicate clusters at matched tokens moves the gap.
- Cheapest test: E27 and A2s at 20M, 2 seeds, uniform vs cluster-weighted sampling (8 runs). (The 1808 review estimated about 8 GPU-min per 20M run; about 1 GPU-h total.) A CPU clustering pass comes first.
- Would change plans: a gap change beyond its CI, with clean80 gaining more than 1%, would make data weighting a goal-1 lever and move C213's mechanism from inference to data.
- Novelty: C200 (dedup) and SQ225 (overlap measurement) do not reweight at matched tokens. SQ315 is packing, not sampling. The 20M screen caveat applies (SQ414: sign kept, size understated).

**C. Start ternary QAT partway through training.**
- Hypothesis (spec): part of the ternary cost (A2s vs A2f +9.5%, C204/C207) is a late-switch effect; starting ternary at 50% of tokens reduces it.
- Code-read: model.py lines 111-112 say BitLinear's quant_on is "used by train.ternary_start_frac". I did not check that the flag is wired in train.py.
- Cheapest test: A2s at 20M, start fraction 0 vs 0.5, 2 seeds (4 runs, about 30 GPU-min).
- Would change plans: a ternary cost under 3% at 20M would make the ternary cost partly a schedule artifact, and the bits-matched question (SQ393) would move up.
- Novelty: no SQ mentions a ternary start fraction (grep). Closest: SQ361 (warm-started tying for loops) and SQ393.

**D. Weight decay sets the ternary flip rate.**
- Hypothesis (spec): AdamW decay on the latent weights controls how often absmean ternary codes flip, and so part of the ternary cost.
- Cheapest test: A2s at 20M, decay at 0, the configured default, and 3x default, 2 seeds (6 runs). I did not read the configured default; it must be checked in gogeta/train.py before the arms are set.
- Would change plans: a spread over 2 points would make the ternary cost partly an optimiser effect, and the A3 decomposition would need a decay term.
- Novelty: no SQ covers weight decay (grep). Closest: SQ11 (Muon vs AdamW) and SQ404 (core LR confound).

**E. Short-to-long length curriculum.**
- Hypothesis (spec): training at 512 tokens, then 1,024, at equal total tokens narrows the long-context copy deficit found in SQ347 (+32% and +37% at gap 256).
- Cheapest test: E1-style at 20M, curriculum vs flat, 2 seeds (4 runs), scored on SQ347's copy probe and clean80.
- Would change plans: if the copy deficit halves with no clean80 loss, SQ383 (coda attention) may be unnecessary and the fix is in the data.
- Novelty: only SQ5 (loop-count curriculum) uses the word. Not proposed: per-pass supervision (SQ5), pass-count robustness (SQ381, SQ403), loops at equal bytes (SQ423).
