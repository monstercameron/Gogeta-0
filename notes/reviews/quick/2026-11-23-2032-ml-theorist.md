## Triage
- Ideas 1 and 2 (local loop stability; in vs out of corpus): accepted and running now as SQ195 on the assembled loop (`experiments/checks/dataflow_loop_gain.py`, A2s, A2f and E27, seeds 2-4, both slices). A first pass with absolute finite-difference gain at eps 1e-3 was discarded as an artifact, for two reasons. It was scale-dependent: E27's near-zero prelude output gets renormalised by the injection, which gave a "gain" of about 875. And it was quantisation-dominated: tiny perturbations cross int8 activation rounding steps in the ternary arms, while full-precision A2f gave sensible values near 1. The rerun uses a scale-free relative gain and an eps sweep (1e-3, 3e-2, 1e-1). Results go in the next version.
- Question (do pass-to-pass gains differ in vs out of corpus?): measured by that rerun; answered next version.
- **Answer (v0.5.57):** the gains (eps >= 3e-2) are below 1 for every pass, so the loop contracts. Out of corpus the A2 models contract less (pass 1: A2f 0.56 in vs 0.68-0.70 out; A2s 0.75-0.78 vs 0.82-0.84), while E27 is the same in and out. Late-pass saturation in E27 is near-identity behaviour (gain trending to 0.80), not contraction to a fixed point. Details in SQ195's row and SQ439.md finding 10.
