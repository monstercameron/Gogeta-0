# SQ410: overlap dose-response of each assembly cost (C213's mechanism)

**Status:** done (exploratory, from SQ239's per-doc re-score) · **Verdict:** the ternary and loop costs grow monotonically with K9 13-gram train overlap, and so does the assembly gain: ternary cost +5.4% (overlap < 0.2) -> +18.1% (>= 0.95), loop cost +1.5% -> +5.6%, assembly gain -13.8% -> -24.9%. The first two support C213's memorisation reading; the third means the assembly gain is also larger on memorised text (its in-corpus -16.7% needs the same caveat), though it stays large (-13.8%) on docs with < 20% overlap · **Links:** `experiments/checks/sq239_analysis.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq410.json`

## Method
Per-doc NLL of the 30 checkpoints on all 4,000 val docs; docs binned by K9 13-gram overlap with train in five bands (0-0.2: 337 docs, 0.2-0.5: 837, 0.5-0.8: 1,159, 0.8-0.95: 788, 0.95-1: 879); paired-by-seed effects per band, t-CI.

## Result (mean [95% CI] by band, n seeds)
| band | ternary cost A2s vs A2f (n=6) | loop cost A2f vs E24 (n=6) | assembly gain A2s vs E27 (n=3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| overlap 0-0.2 | +5.37 [+4.65, +6.09] | +1.48 [+1.00, +1.96] | -13.75 [-15.28, -12.21] |
| 0.2-0.5 | +9.50 | +2.99 | -16.23 |
| 0.5-0.8 | +13.69 | +4.69 | -19.47 |
| 0.8-0.95 | +16.41 | +5.61 | -22.44 |
| 0.95-1 | +18.07 [+17.53, +18.62] | +5.59 [+4.95, +6.22] | -24.90 [-25.53, -24.26] |
Slopes (points per band): +3.23 (ternary), +1.08 (loop), -2.85 (assembly gain).

## Reading and caveats
- Costs fall toward the out-of-corpus values as overlap falls (SQ400: +0.4% ternary, -1.9% loop), but the lowest in-corpus band still shows +5.4% and +1.5%: the dose-response is smooth; extrapolated to zero overlap the loop cost is near the out-of-corpus value (+1.5 vs -1.9) and the ternary cost stays above it (+5.4 vs +0.4). The overlap band is confounded with document length and source (high-overlap docs are mostly the near-duplicate algorithmic docs).
- The assembly gain growing with overlap is a limitation of the C209 wording: -13.8% on low-overlap docs is the more conservative in-corpus number, SQ400's -11.3% out of corpus the most conservative. Descriptive; K9 overlap is a 13-gram measure, not a semantic one.
