## Question

- Do score artifacts record the tokenizer identity as well as the validation-document identity?
## Triage
- Tokenizer and token-stream integrity: accepted as SQ418. It is a real gap. The v2 manifest pins `tokenizer.json`'s sha256 (0e75f39f...), but the fleet's pre-training check hashes only the split files (train.bin, val.bin, docs_val.jsonl, train_keep_ids.npy), and score files record only `docs_val_sha256` and `overlap_sha256`. SQ418 adds the tokenizer hash to both, plus a 1,000-doc re-encode round trip against the stored streams.
- Question (do score artifacts record tokenizer identity as well as document identity?): not today. They record the validation-document identity (`docs_val_sha256`, the SQ345 join key) and the overlap list's hash, but not the tokenizer. The tokenizer is pinned only indirectly: `val.bin` and `train.bin` hashes match the manifest, and those streams were produced by the pinned tokenizer. SQ418 makes it explicit.
