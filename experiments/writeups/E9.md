## What it will test, in plain English

> **Is it the *loop* that hurts, or something else?** [E2](E2.html) (the looped model) did worse than the others, but it differed in two ways at once: it reuses the same layers several times, *and* it has a different set of layers. E9 takes E2's exact layers and runs each **once**, with no reuse and no loop.
>
> - If E9 beats E2: looping those layers is genuinely worse than just running them once.
> - If E2 beats E9: looping *is* buying something, it's just expensive.
