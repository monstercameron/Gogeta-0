Self-speculative decoding

Powering up recorded in v0.5.20

The question: Draft with 1–2 loops, verify with full r: what is the acceptance rate, and the real speedup on the laptop?

What it will test, in plain English

Use the loop to type faster. A looped model can make a quick rough guess of the next few words using only 1–2 passes, then check all of them at once with the full number of passes. Correct guesses are kept, and wrong ones are thrown away and redone. The output is identical to normal generation, just faster.
