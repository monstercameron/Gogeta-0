# SQ400: registered out-of-corpus test of "the ternary and loop costs are mostly memorisation" (C213)

**Status:** done (registered in PREDICTIONS.md section SQ400, with the main agent's decision rule quoted verbatim) · **Verdict:** every registered prediction P1-P7 holds, and the decision rule fires: with paired-by-seed 95% CIs over 6 seeds, the out-of-corpus ternary cost (A2s vs A2f) is +0.42% [-0.92, +1.77] (clean80-300: +8.94% [+8.31, +9.58]) and the out-of-corpus loop cost (A2f vs E24) is -1.90% [-3.40, -0.40], i.e. looped A2f is better than the unlooped GDN hybrid out of corpus (clean80-300: +2.49% [+1.79, +3.19]). By the rule C213 changes design priorities · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq400_ooc_registered.py`, results `experiments/checks/results/sq400.json` (both intervals, per-seed effects, slices, rule evaluation); SQ379 scores re-used for seeds 2-4

## Method
200M-token checkpoints at best LR, seeds 2-7 where they exist: A2s, A2f, E20 (2-7), E24 (2-7; seed 3 was pulled meanwhile), E27 (2-4 only), A3mx (5-7). Scored with the CPU scorer (fp32, K8 windowing, window 1024) on (1) the SQ99 out-of-corpus set (330 library files, sha256-verified), (2) its stdlib (n = see JSON) and site-packages slices (a held-out-by-source slice; the locked `code-py-v1-test` set was not touched), (3) the 300-doc clean80 subset. Effect = mean over paired seeds of 100 x (bpc_arm / bpc_base - 1) on aggregate bits/char; paired-by-seed t-interval (df = n - 1, includes seed variance, used for the rules) and the package-clustered bootstrap over documents (B = 2000; document sampling only).

## Reading and caveats
- The in-corpus costs are not out-of-corpus costs: the ternary cost goes from +8.9% to about +0.4%, the loop cost from +2.5% to -1.9%, the fusion cost from +28.7% to +14.8%. Consistent with "mostly in-distribution capacity or memorisation", but also with a compression of ratios at the 6-7x higher loss level out of corpus; the rule does not separate those, and a dense model also loses its edge: A2f is +1.5% worse than dense out of corpus while -0.7% in corpus.
- The out-of-corpus set is raw library code (a distribution shift beyond held-out repositories); the stdlib slice is small, and its ternary estimate (+1.38%, CI above 0) shows the cost is not exactly zero everywhere.
- Intervals: paired-by-seed CIs have df = 2-5 (n = 3-6) and are wide; the package-clustered intervals are narrower and ignore seed variance (P1's cluster CI excludes 0 at +0.05 to +0.76 while the paired-by-seed one includes 0). E27 and A3mx only have 3 seeds each. Descriptive beyond the registered rules.
