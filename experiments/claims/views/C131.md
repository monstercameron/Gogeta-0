## Limitations

- Measured on two 4,000-doc samples (val and a pseudo-val of held-out train docs) with token 13-grams; 'any random split leaks' is an inference from the corpus-level duplication for doc-level random splits, not a test of every split; 13-grams miss paraphrases (K9 card caveats) [event 3, v0.4.59]
- SQ225: the overlap is concentrated by source config: 52.3% of algorithmic_corpus val docs have >= 80% 13-gram overlap vs 22.9% of synthetic_code_snippet (diff +29.3 pts, bootstrap CI 26.6-32.2). The val/train config mix itself matches (63.9% vs 62.9% algorithmic, chi2 1.70). Clean-val results are therefore weighted toward synthetic_code_snippet; per-config stratified scores are the follow-up. [event 4, v0.4.76]
