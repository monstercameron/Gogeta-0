### 2026-11-22 21:2x · Result (CPU, seed 0, commit f7a7aa7)
The 330 sq_99 files were re-read at their recorded paths; sha256 of the normalised text matched for 330/330 and token counts for 330/330 (no files missing). Repeat share = fraction of token 8-gram positions already seen earlier in the doc (mean 0.118, p90 0.253; correlation with log length 0.476). Outcome y = 100*(nll_E1/nll_E0 - 1) per doc. OLS on z-scored covariates, bootstrap over the 31 packages (B=4000, percentile).
- log length alone: b 1.55 [1.15, 1.98], R2 0.204 (1.42 pct points per doubling).
- repeat share alone: b 1.43 [0.87, 1.96], R2 0.173.
- joint: log length 1.13 [0.75, 1.56]; repeat 0.89 [0.28, 1.43]; R2 0.256 (1.03 pct points per doubling).
- repeat terciles: mean y -1.84 / -0.24 / +1.18 (median length 577 / 1267 / 1594 tokens).
Reading: E1's disadvantage grows with both; the length slope survives conditioning, so "repetition replaces length" is not supported, though length and repetition cannot be fully separated at r = 0.48 with 31 packages.
