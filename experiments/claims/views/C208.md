## Limitations

- Matched on active training FLOPs, not wall-clock: A3mx trains at 288k tok/s vs E24 368k and E20 586k (dense attention uses fused kernels), so per wall-clock it trails further. Per weight memory (ternary at 1.58 bits) it is the smallest: 44.0 MB vs E24 77.2 MB and E20 74.7 MB. [event 3, v0.5.17]
- One horizon (200M tokens of code-py-v2): the F2 interaction flipped sign between 20M and 200M (C201 -> C202), so the gap to E24 needs a longer-horizon point (SQ380). [event 4, v0.5.17]
- E20 is not FLOP-matched: 4.01e16 training FLOPs vs A3mx's 3.79e16 (+6%, a3_runs.json 'flops'). The '+1.1% vs E20' is an inconclusive point estimate (95% CI [-0.70, +2.93], df 2), not evidence of equivalence or of 'does not beat' in a TOST sense; it only means no advantage over dense was shown. [event 6, v0.5.18]
- The weight-memory figures (A3mx 44.0 MB at 1.58 bits per ternary weight) assume ideal 2-bit packing that has not been built or measured (BitLinear fake-quantises in bf16; SQ378). Training-time ternary is simulated, so any inference-cost reading of ternary is analytic. [event 8, v0.5.19]
- A2s's best bracketed LR is 3e-3 (0.58% better than 6e-3 at seed 5), so effects measured against A2s at 6e-3 may be overstated by up to about 0.6 points; no registered decision changes (every fix stays beyond -1%). [event 10, v0.5.23]
