## 2. Critique

**2.1 The headline arms differ in gradient flow, and that is not in any registered confound list (code-read, computed).** model.py:19 says r is sampled uniformly in [loops_min, loops_max] and that only the last bptt_loops passes are backpropagated. model.py:364-367 run the earlier passes under no_grad, so their outputs enter the last passes as constants. E27 has loops_min 1, loops_max 7, bptt 4 (e27_fact111.json). Per step, E[max(0, r-4)] = (0+0+0+0+1+2+3)/7 = 0.857 detached applications, against E[r] = 4 applications, so 21% of E27's core applications are forward-only. A2s (r = 2, bptt 2) and A1g/F-6 (r in 1..3, bptt 4) have none (a1g.json, a2s.json). So the arm that anchors C209 is the only one whose early trajectory is not trained for the readout. SQ19 and the 1918 BPTT-matched idea cover the test; the mechanism (detached state entering the last passes) is what the registered text omits.

**2.2 The logit lens reads states that were never trained to be read (code-read, quoted).** The loss is computed once, on the final logits (model.py:386-389). Intermediate h_k are never supervised. SQ348's "per-pass readout improves up to r = 4" therefore reads the final head on states it was not fit to decode, so the curve may show the head's fit to pass-4 features rather than what each pass holds. SQ348 should be read as a lens, not a probe. Any per-pass claim needs an auxiliary head (SQ207 arm D) before it is stated.

**2.3 Design choices made by loss alone, without inspecting the signal (code-read).**
- model.py:79 (`ternary_acts` = True) and :124-125 (per-token absmax int8): the clip and zero rates were never measured. SQ339 measures int8 only post hoc and in aggregate, on E-F2 cells 000 and 100 (+0.1%).
- model.py:120-121: one absmean scale per matrix. Per-channel scales are never compared.
- model.py:368: the re-injected prelude output is RMS-normalised (norm(e)), so its absolute scale is discarded. No arm removes this step.
- model.py:78 vs :370-371: non-contractive injection is the default. Contractive injection was adopted from the A1 screen (8 arms, seeds 0-1).
- model.py:255: the decay g has no bound, and its activation-dependent values have never been logged (SQ330 uses weights only).
- model.py:49 (coda = [attn]) and :54 (window 256): window 256 was kept after SQ214 (exploratory, 4 seeds) found it beats full context by 1.3%.

**2.4 The coda reads an un-normalised state (code-read, speculation on the effect).** Coda blocks add to the raw h (model.py:380-382). Only the output is normalised (:386). SQ30 quotes ||h_k|| at about 4.6-5 times ||h_0|| (wording ambiguous). (spec) The coda's additions are then a smaller share of the logits at higher pass counts. Q21 and SQ31 V1 (core-end RMSNorm) already queue the fix. The diagnosis, a per-pass share, is not measured anywhere.

**2.5 Missing measurements.** No activation-range census exists in the repo: grep for outlier, amax, kurtosis and fp8 finds only hardware peaks (SQ71). The int8 and fp8 safety of the ternary path is therefore untested beyond SQ339. C19's failure (16 of 120 runs, DEVLOG v0.5.20 and v0.5.53) is a near-tie effect and was not traced to activation ranges.

**2.6 Seeds and multiplicity.** Two-seed "no difference" readings remain common (SQ401, SQ402). C206 and C212 contain selection seeds, and the A1 screen has 8 arms on 2 seeds. These were triaged in 1808 and 1937, so they are not repeated here.

## 3. Alternative ideas (speculation unless quoted or computed)

**I1. Injection share and the scale of e (model.py:368, :370).**
- Hypothesis (spec): the share of the re-injected signal in h falls with pass index, and that fall, not the loop itself, sets the over-looping penalty (C7).
- Computed: take ||h_4|| = 5 ||h_0|| (SQ30, quoted, ambiguous) with linear growth ||h_k|| = 1 + k·δ, so δ = 1. The injection share δ/||h_k|| is then 1/2 at pass 1 and 1/5 at pass 4.
- Cheapest test (CPU, about 20 min): on the A2s and E27 seed-2 checkpoints, 64 clean80 docs, log per-pass ||inj||/||h_k||. Then one eval-only arm: rescale e to its training RMS in place of norm(e).
- Result that changes plans: if the share is under 0.25 by pass 4 and the rescaled-e arm lowers loss at r = 4, an e-scale parameter joins E61's candidate list. If neither, drop it.
- Novelty: closest are SQ329 (eval-time injection gain on E2, still an idea) and SQ30 (increment ratio). Neither measures the share or tests norm(e).

**I2. Per-projection ternary error budget (model.py:118-127).**
- Hypothesis (spec): most of C207's ~9.5% sits in one or two projections, most likely the SwiGLU down-projection (input silu(a)*b, heavy-tailed) and the inject, so per-projection precision recovers most of the gain at a small bit cost.
- Computed: for Gaussian weights, the absmean rule (model.py:120) zeroes P(|w| < 0.5·E|w|) = 2Φ(0.399) - 1 = 31% of weights. That is the reference for the trained zero rates.
- Cheapest test (CPU, about 30 min): on the A2f checkpoint, per projection: ||W - Q(W)||/||W||, the output error ||Wx - Q(W)Q(x)||/||Wx|| on 64 docs, and the input amax/rms.
- Result that changes plans: if one projection type carries over half the output error, E61 gets a per-projection precision map. If the error is spread evenly, per-projection precision is dropped.
- Novelty: SQ339 (aggregate), SQ192 (training split) and C207 (two groups) exist. Per-projection output error on the assembled model is new.

**I3. Activation-range census and int8 zeroing (model.py:124-125).**
- Hypothesis (spec): per-token absmax int8 is safe on average (SQ339), but a few outlier channels set the step for all 384 channels, and C19's near-tie failures concentrate on those tokens.
- Computed: for Gaussian channels, amax over 384 values is about 3σ, so step/2 = 3σ/254 ≈ 0.012σ and about 0.9% of elements round to zero. With one 10x outlier, amax ≈ 30σ, step/2 ≈ 0.12σ and about 9.4% round to zero.
- Cheapest test (CPU, about 20 min): on the same checkpoints, per BitLinear input: amax/rms and zero rate per token, and the NLL change from quant on/off, binned by zero rate.
- Result that changes plans: if tokens with zero rate above 5% carry most of the NLL change, E61 (24 GB cards) gets a per-group scale or clipping arm. Otherwise int8 is safe and C19's failure is a batch-invariance problem.
- Novelty: SQ339, SQ407 and SQ71 do not measure ranges or zero rates.

**I4. A learned readout over pass outputs (model.py:380-381).**
- Hypothesis (spec): the coda reads only the final h, so it cannot use early-pass states. A learned softmax mix over h_1..h_r (r parameters per model) lets it, and removes the penalty beyond r = 4.
- Cheapest test (GPU, after the current batch): one 20M E2-layout arm with the mix and one plain r = 4 arm, 2 seeds each. Score at r = 4, 8 and 12. Computed cost: 4 runs at about 3.5 GPU-min each (B1 rate quoted in 1937), about 14 GPU-min.
- Result that changes plans: if the mix arm at r = 8 and 12 matches r = 4, the penalty is a readout problem and E5 gets a cheaper form. If the mix does not help, the penalty is in the state, and SQ15 and SQ31 are the right fix.
- Novelty: SQ348 (read-only lens), SQ92 (oracle loop count) and SQ230 (representation probe) do not train a readout. None is listed as an arm.

**I5. Activation-dependent GDN decay (model.py:237-240, :255).**
- Hypothesis (spec): heads spend capacity in one regime (near-identity memory or near-full forgetting). A head budget or a fixed decay per head then costs little.
- Computed from the init ranges: |g| = A·dt, with A ~ U(1,16) and dt ~ logU(0.001, 0.1) (model.py:237-239), so |g| is in [0.001, 1.6] and the half-life ln2/|g| runs from about 0.4 to 690 tokens at init (a(x) small).
- Cheapest test (CPU, about 15 min): on E1 and A2s seed-2 checkpoints, 64 clean80 docs, log exp(g_t) per head and token. Report the share of head-tokens with exp(g) above 0.99 and below 0.5.
- Result that changes plans: if a third or more of head-tokens sit near identity in some heads, a per-head budget or fixed-decay arm joins E61's candidates.
- Novelty: SQ330 (weights-only half-lives) is the closest and this extends it to activations. SQ235 is the delta-rule ablation, not decay usage.

**I6. A head census before SQ383 (model.py:49, :386).**
- Hypothesis (spec): the coda attention's copy role sits in one or two heads. SQ383's zero-the-coda test is then either a no-op or a whole-layer loss, and a head-level test would separate them.
- Cheapest test (CPU, about 15 min): per coda head, the share of attention mass beyond the 256-token window, on clean80 docs of at least 512 tokens.
- Result that changes plans: if every head keeps over 95% of its mass inside 256 tokens, SQ383 and the coda copy story are dropped before any run. If one or two heads hold the far mass, SQ383 becomes a head-level ablation.
- Novelty: SQ383 (layer-level eval zeroing), SQ191 and SQ397 are layer-level, not per head.
