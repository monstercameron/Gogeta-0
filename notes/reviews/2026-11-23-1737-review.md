## 2. Critique

**2.1 The headline is not parameter-matched (code-read, computed; this challenges the 1710 triage).** E27 (`experiments/staged-configs/e27_fact111.json`) has a 3-block core run at a random r in 1..7. A2s (`experiments/staged-configs/a2/a2s.json`) has a 6-block core run at a fixed r = 2. A1a, the comparator in triage item D, has the same 3-block core as E27, at r = 4. All three reach 14 block applications, so FLOPs match, but unique non-embedding parameters do not. At d = 384 and mlp 1024 a block is about 1.9M parameters (computed; consistent with C197's 26-27M for 14 unshared blocks). That gives about 9.5M unique stack parameters for E27 and A1a (1 + 3 + 1 blocks) and about 15M for A2s (1 + 6 + 1). C197 says unique parameters are the lever at matched FLOPs. So fixing r (A1a) does not remove the largest difference between the arms. The "about 12.5 of 16.7 points survive, so mostly the assembly" reading is not shown.

**2.2 The same confound sits in the loop cost (code-read, computed).** A2f vs E24 (`experiments/staged-configs/e24_fact100.json`, 14 unshared blocks, about 27M) is matched on FLOPs, but A2f has about 15M unique parameters. The "+3.2% loop cost" (C213's registration) and the C214 reversal are therefore not separable from a unique-parameter effect in either direction.

**2.3 The compensations were screened on the old shape (code-read).** The A1 configs (`experiments/staged-configs/a1/`) use the 3-block core; A2 moves the winners onto a 6-block core at r = 2 and stacks them. Their effects were never measured on the 6-block shape, so "one change at a time" held only on the old shape.

**2.4 Init may be a hidden factor for the recipe (quoted SQ308, code-read).** SQ308 found the 14-block edge over E1 depends on depth-scaled init (2.6% with it, none with flat). That was tested on E32 only. A2s and E27 both use the default `gogeta` init (model.py `_init_weights`), and nothing tests init on the assembled shape.

**2.5 Metric aggregation is untested for the headline (spec).** The headlines use doc-level clean80 bpc. A per-document mean and a token-weighted mean can rank arms differently when document lengths vary, and C193 found a length dependence for E1 (quoted). I found no token-weighted number for C209 in the ledger. A CPU re-aggregation of `experiments/stats/doclevel/` would settle it.

**2.6 Seed bars are uneven across claims (quoted).** C209 is VERIFIED on n = 3 seeds at one horizon. C5 was refuted under a 7-seed rule, and C198 and C199 are PARTIAL on 2-4 seeds. A recipe headline on 3 seeds and a 0.2% difference on 7 seeds are judged by different bars. AGENTS section 0 should state the bar per claim type (spec on the bar; the seed counts are quoted).

**2.7 Init depth is coupled to the eval default (code-read, low risk now).** `effective_depth()` uses `cfg.loops`, not the realised r. E27 and A1i (random r, loops = 4) and A2s (loops = 2) each happen to give depth 14, so no current result is affected. A future config that changes the default loop count would change the init scale without changing the model. A one-line assertion would catch it.

## 3. Alternative ideas (all speculation)

**A. Parameter-matched control for the headline.** Hypothesis (spec): at matched FLOPs and matched unique parameters, most of the 16% goes away. Define F-6 as the E27 recipe (full fusion, random r) with a 6-block core (gdn, gdn, swa, gdn, gdn, swa), r ~ U{1..3} (mean 2, so 12 block applications like E27 and A2s), no contractive injection and no fixed r. Arms: E27, F-6, A2s; 20M tokens, 3 seeds, 9 runs. Cheapest test: about 1.5 GPU-hours on one 5090 (spec; check against recent 20M run times). Plan-changing result: if F-6 closes at least half of the E27-to-A2s gap, the headline is mainly a unique-parameter result and C209's wording changes. If F-6 is close to E27, the gap belongs to the compensations at matched shape. Novelty: C197 and SQ282 test unique parameters for unshared stacks; no queued item has a 6-block control for the assembled recipe. The 20M horizon is a sign screen only (SQ414, C201).

**B. Ternary at matched weight bytes, the goal-6 metric.** Hypothesis (spec): at a fixed packed-weight budget, a ternary model with about 3x the parameters beats a bf16 model. Cheapest test: computed width matching (1.58 vs 16 bits per weight, quoted from SQ378's 16.0x packed size), then A2s and a bf16 arm at about the same bytes, 20M tokens, 2 seeds, roughly 25 GPU-min (spec). Plan-changing result: if the bf16 arm wins at equal bytes, ternary is a kernel-speed bet only, and goal 6 leans fp16. Novelty: C207 scores ternary at full-precision FLOPs and SQ378 measured size only; no bytes-matched arm is queued.

**C. Which unique parameters carry the lever.** Hypothesis (spec): the extra unique parameters help mostly through the MLPs, not the mixers. Cheapest test: A2s at 20M, 2 seeds, with the mixers frozen at random init (MLPs and norms train), and the reverse, about 8 GPU-min per arm. Plan-changing result: if freezing the mixers costs little, the width and MLP axis (SQ243, SQ241) matters more than the GDN story, and the mixer claims weaken. Novelty: SQ91 changes mixers, it does not freeze them; no freeze test is queued.

**D. Token-weighted re-aggregation (zero GPU).** Hypothesis (spec): the C209 gap survives token-weighted aggregation. Cheapest test: read `experiments/stats/doclevel/` for E27 and A2s (seeds 2-4), recompute clean80 per-document mean and token-weighted bpc, and bootstrap the gap; minutes on CPU. Plan-changing result: if the gap moves outside its CI half-width, the headline needs a metric qualifier. Novelty: C193 checks length for E1 only; no token-weighted C209 number exists.

**E. Init on the assembled shape.** Hypothesis (spec): the flat init narrows the A2s-to-E27 gap, as SQ308 found for E32. Cheapest test: E27 and A2s each with `init: flat`, 20M, 2 seeds, about 20 GPU-min. Plan-changing result: a large change in the gap means the headline depends on an init choice and needs a sensitivity line in C209. Novelty: SQ308 tested E32 only, and SQ200 tested dense stacks.
