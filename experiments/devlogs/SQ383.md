# SQ383: is the coda attention carrying copy-at-distance? (A2s)

**Status:** done (descriptive, not registered; no prediction registered for this probe) · **Verdict:** yes. Replacing the single full-attention coda block of A2s by 256-token sliding-window attention at eval leaves copy at gap 128 untouched (0.0%: the window covers it) but removes the copy gain at gaps 256 and 512 (second-copy NLL +41.9% and +13.3%, to the no-copy level), while clean80 bits/char moves only +2.76% · **Links:** script `experiments/checks/sq383_coda_attention_copy.py` (imports the SQ347 probe), results `experiments/checks/results/sq383.json`

## Method
A2s (200M, ternary assembled; core 6 blocks, loops 2; prelude swa; coda one full-attention block), seeds 2, 3, 4, best LR 6e-3. At eval time only: (full) baseline; (zero) the coda attention branch outputs 0 (MLP and residual kept); (swa256) the coda attention uses window 256 instead of full causal. Then the SQ347 probe: the same 200 synthetic sequences per gap (128, 256, 512; a 32-token span repeated after the gap), NLL of the second copy and of the first occurrence, and clean80 bits/char on the 300-doc subset. Nothing is retrained, so the ablation mixes "this attention carries copy" with "the rest depends on it".

## Result (n = 3 seeds; mean change vs full, 95% t-CI, df 2)
| arm | copy NLL change gap 128 | gap 256 | gap 512 | clean80-300 bits/char change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| swa256 | 0.0% [0.0, 0.0] | +41.9% [+34.9, +48.9] | +13.3% [+7.8, +18.8] | +2.76% [+2.26, +3.27] |
| zero | +51.4% [+44.2, +58.5] | +42.6% [+35.6, +49.6] | +14.2% [+9.4, +19.1] | +72.1% [+60.5, +83.7] |
Absolute second-copy NLL (nats/token), seeds 2/3/4, full: gap 128 2.107/2.134/2.176, 256 2.392/2.446/2.513, 512 3.112/3.186/3.223; swa256: 128 identical, 256 3.470/3.417/3.541, 512 3.604/3.556/3.627. First-occurrence (no-copy) NLL for comparison: 3.25/3.20/3.25 (128), 3.28/3.20/3.26 (256), 3.42/3.37/3.42 (512): so at gaps 256 and 512 the swa256 model's copy NLL equals or exceeds the no-copy level (3.4-3.5 vs 3.2-3.3 at gap 256), i.e. all of the copy gain is gone.

## Reading and caveats
- The coda's full attention is what carries copy beyond the 256 window in A2s: the SWA blocks of the core cannot, and the GDN state does not compensate. This supports "copy at distance needs full attention OUTSIDE the loop" (the SQ347 deficit of the GDN cells), but the ablation shows the model's use of the coda, not what a model with better placed attention would do.
- Zeroing the coda attention breaks the model (+72% bits/char), so its copy numbers are uninformative; swa256 is the clean ablation (+2.8% bpc, copy specifically lost beyond the window). At gap 512 the full model's own copy gain is small (3.1-3.2 vs 3.4 no-copy), so the +13% is a smaller absolute effect than at 256.
- n = 3 seeds (df 2); sequences are the 200-per-gap synthetic set of SQ347. Descriptive.
