# SQ439: data-flow atlas of the assembled model (+ SQ441, the first-layer uniform swap)

**Status:** done (descriptive, v0.5.55) · **Why:** the human asked (2026-11-23 ~20:10) for the data passed between layers to be measured and refined into new techniques. Until now every component had been judged by end-to-end loss only. · **Scripts:** `experiments/checks/dataflow_atlas.py` (+ `dataflow_atlas_summary.py`), `experiments/checks/dataflow_uniform_swa.py` · **Outputs:** `results/dataflow_atlas.json`, `results/dataflow_atlas_summary.md`, `results/dataflow_uniform_swa.json`

**Setup:**
- A2s, A2f, E27, E24 and E20 at 200M tokens, seeds 2-4 (15 checkpoints), on CPU in fp32 with GDN on the torch reference kernel.
- 12 packed windows of 1,024 tokens from in-corpus clean80 (v1 val docs, seeded subset) and 12 from the SQ99 out-of-corpus files.
- **Caveat:** logit-lens CE of an intermediate state is a lens, not a probe. Only the final logits are trained (model.py:386-389), so a lens on an untrained-to-decode state overstates its loss. The final CE is the model's real next-token loss on these windows: in corpus A2s 0.805, A2f 0.731, E27 0.933, E24 0.711, E20 0.753 nats per token; out of corpus 3.49, 3.53, 3.95, 3.57, 3.45.

## Findings

1. **The first sliding-window layer is a causal moving average, and its selective part only serves in-corpus recall (SQ441).**
   - In E24 and A2f, every head of the first SWA layer has entropy 5.28-5.30 nats and a mean distance of about 111 tokens. That is uniform attention over the causal 256-token window at T = 1,024.
   - Swapping that layer's attention for the exact uniform window average (same v and o projections, no queries or keys) changes next-token CE as follows:

     | arm | in corpus | out of corpus |
     |---|---|---|
     | E24 | 0.00% (all 3 seeds) | 0.00% |
     | A2f | +0.6 to +0.9% | +0.03 to +0.07% |
     | A2s | +3.0 to +5.5% | +0.1 to +0.4% |
     | E27 | +6.5 to +7.3% | -0.1 to +0.1% |

   - Out of corpus, the first layer's attention selectivity is worth essentially nothing in every arm. In corpus, the ternary-heavy models lean on it for up to 7%.
   - This is independent evidence, from inside the network, for C213's reading that the assembly costs and gains are partly in-corpus recall.
2. **E27's ternary prelude is nearly inert.**
   - Its residual RMS goes from 0.09 (embedding) to only 0.14 after the prelude block, and the lens CE stays near the embedding's (32.8 nats).
   - Pass 1's input is therefore entirely the injection (injection share 1.00).
   - In A2s, whose ends are full precision, the prelude writes a 5.6-RMS representation; its MLP write is 66x the embedding's norm.
   - So "full-precision ends" (A1e, -7.6%) works at least partly because a ternary first block cannot build the representation from the tiny embedding. That is a mechanism, not only a recipe.
3. **Loop passes past the second do little.**
   - E27, r = 4: pass changes are 2.04 (pass 1), 0.56, 0.32, 0.22 of the input norm. Pass-to-pass cosine is 0.984, 0.996, 0.998, and lens CE goes 1.150 -> 1.058 -> 1.033 -> 1.024.
   - A2s, r = 2: pass 1 changes the state by 1.38x (output-vs-input cosine 0.58); pass 2 refines (0.73x, cosine 0.93).
   - Out of corpus, A2s's pass 2 moves the lens CE only 6.67 -> 6.55 (in corpus 2.15 -> 1.85).
4. **The injection's share falls with each pass.**
   - A2s: 0.33 (pass 1) and 0.20 (pass 2), with contractive gate lam mean 0.75 (p05 0.64, p95 0.87).
   - A2f: 0.23 and 0.11.
   - E27: 1.00, 0.30, 0.18, 0.13. The residual RMS grows 3.3 -> 25 over 4 passes, so each re-injection is a shrinking fraction of the state.
5. **The coda (one full-attention block) does heavy lifting, most of all out of corpus.**
   - A2s's coda writes 0.47x (mixer) and 0.71x (MLP) of the residual, and the lens CE falls 1.85 -> 0.81 in corpus and 6.55 -> 3.49 out of corpus.
   - Its output's effective rank collapses to about 30 (A2s, A2f), against 90 for E27 and about 50 at the last layer of E24/E20.
   - The coda's full attention looks back 75-130 tokens on average, at T = 1,024.
6. **Ternary weight error is about Gaussian in the core and worst at E27's ends.**
   - Relative output error vs the layer's own latent weights is 0.50-0.56 in the core for every projection type.
   - At E27's ends it is coda attention output 0.83, prelude qkv 0.72, coda MLP down 0.69 and inject 0.64.
   - This metric measures distance from the latents (the model trained through the quantised path), not damage, but it locates the projections where ternary fits worst.
7. **int8 activations are safe; the down-projections hold the outliers.**
   - The activation-quantisation part of the error is 0.4-2.6%, largest at the MLP down-projection inputs (crest factor 11-19, vs 3-4 elsewhere).
   - C19's losslessness failure is therefore unlikely to be a range problem; the near-tie and batch-invariance explanation stands.
8. **GDN hybrids keep a better-conditioned residual stream than dense attention.**
   - Dense E20 builds anisotropy up to 0.37 and crest about 10 in its middle layers (outlier features).
   - The GDN stack E24 stays at 0.05-0.08 anisotropy and crest about 3.7 throughout. That is a reason GDN and low-precision activations fit together.
9. **GDN heads are multi-scale.** Median decay horizons per head span about 7 to over 2,000 tokens in every GDN layer, and write strength beta is 0.44-0.78.

## Techniques these suggest (side quests)

- **SQ442, attention-free first layer:** replace the first SWA layer's attention with a causal window mean, or an EMA, at training time. It removes q/k projections and attention compute. The eval-only swap already costs 0.0% (E24) and at most 0.4% out of corpus.
- **SQ443, a per-projection precision map:** keep ternary where the error is Gaussian-like (core) and full precision only where it is worst (coda attention output, prelude qkv, coda down, inject). This is finer than "full-precision ends" and saves bits.
- **SQ444, an output gain for ternary ends:** a learnable per-channel fp scale (no weight decay) after BitLinear in the prelude, testing whether E27's inert prelude is a gain problem.
- **SQ445, pass budget by marginal change:** a halting or early-exit rule from the measured pass change (passes past 2 change the state by <= 0.3x), and pass 2 as a low-rank adapter pass (`loop_adapter_rank` exists, unused).
- **SQ446, coda capacity:** a second coda block, or a wider coda MLP, at matched parameters, since the coda carries the largest share out of corpus.
- **SQ440 (already added), learned readout over pass outputs.**
