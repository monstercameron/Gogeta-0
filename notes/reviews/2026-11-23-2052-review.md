## 2. Critique

**2.1 C215 is over-claimed as worded.** Its proposition calls three changes "the A2 compensations": fixed r = 2, a ternary core with full-precision prelude, coda and inject, and contractive injection. C1 tested only the bundle. The precision change alone is large: C205 (A1e, E27 with full-precision ends, seeds 2-4, 200M) is -7.56%, about the size of the whole bundle's -6.85%. Without an F-6-plus-full-precision-ends arm, the ~7% cannot be split into precision and everything else. What is established is "the bundle beats F-6 at matched unique params and FLOPs", not "the compensations improve the recipe". Per AGENTS 0 the wording should change: add a limitation event now, and open a new, narrower claim (definitions are immutable).

**2.2 Bits are not matched, and the gap is large.** (computed) Block shapes from model.py (d = 384, mlp 1024, 6 heads): an attention or SWA block is about 1.77M params, a GDN block about 1.93M. The A2s unique total is about 15.04M, of which prelude + coda + inject is about 3.83M (25.5%) and stays full precision. Storing ternary at 1.58 bit and full precision at 16 bit, non-embedding: A2s about 9.9 MB, F-6 about 3.0 MB, ratio about 3.3. Inputs: 11.2M core x 1.58 bit + 3.83M x 16 bit, against 15.04M x 1.58 bit for F-6. Norms and GDN gate terms are ignored. The README's headline metric is "quality per bit of weight memory and per FLOP", yet no result reports per bit (SQ264 is an idea). So C215 is a quality-at-matched-params win bought with about 3x the weight bytes. The limitation text says "stores more weight bytes" without a number.

**2.3 S = 0.61 labels four things as "shape and params".** (computed) The arithmetic is right: -0.11137 / -0.18233 = 0.611 (sq419.json mean_log). But R1 (F-6 vs E27) changes:
- unique params and core shape (E27: 3 unique blocks, mean r = 4; F-6: 6 blocks, mean r = 2);
- loop range (E27 r ~ U{1..7}; F-6 r ~ U{1..3});
- BPTT truncation (E27 has bptt 4 with r up to 7: E[max(0, r-4)] = (0+0+0+0+1+2+3)/7 = 0.857 detached applications per step, 21% of its mean 4; F-6 has none). This is the 2007 finding and it is not in the registered decomposition;
- eval depth (E27 scored at r = 4, F-6 at r = 2; sq419.json "eval_depth").
C212 puts the random-vs-fixed r effect at 3.2% at E27. (spec) If it were additive, up to about 3.2/10.5 = 30% of R1 could be the sampling policy. S's interval is also the spread of three seeds (per-seed t, df 2), not the uncertainty of the design.

**2.4 The SQ441 reading overreaches.** (computed) A uniform causal window of 256 at T = 1,024 has mean entropy 5.299 nats and mean attention distance 111.6 tokens. The reported E24 and A2f values (5.28-5.30 nats, about 111 tokens) match that exactly. So those heads are uniform by construction, and the E24 0.00% swap checks the implementation, not selectivity. The informative arms are A2f (+0.6 to 0.9%), A2s (+3.0 to 5.5%) and E27 (+6.5 to 7.3%). The in-corpus cost rises with how ternary the core is, which recall does not predict on its own. The zero out-of-corpus cost favours recall, but an eval-only swap cannot separate recall from downstream layers that were trained on the exact learned output. SQ442 (trained) is the test. Also, SQ439 reuses the same 15 checkpoints and the SQ99 files behind C213 and C214, so calling it "independent evidence" for C213 overstates it.

**2.5 C1 controls the wrong axis for the width question.** It matches A2s and F-6 to each other. C209's open question is whether the recipe beats E27 at E27's parameters. That is SQ433, still an idea and not queued. Both controls are needed: C1 says the bundle helps at matched shape, SQ433 says the recipe helps at matched width.

**2.6 Minor.** C215 uses three fresh seeds, one LR seed (seed 5), and a paired interval with df 2. The bracket shows 6e-3 within 0.12% of F-6's best and 0.58% of A2s's best, but A2s's best LR at 200M was 3e-3 in C206's bracket, so LR is a live confound for any new arm.

## 3. Alternative ideas (speculation unless quoted or computed)

**I1. Bit-matched control for C215 (precision scope).**
- Hypothesis (spec): F-6 with full-precision prelude, coda and inject recovers at least 70% of A2s's gain over F-6. If so, most of C215 is precision scope, not the fixed-r or contractive parts.
- Cheapest test: 20M tokens, seeds 2-4, arms F-6, F-6 with ternary_fp_ends, and A2s. Nine runs at about 3.5 GPU-min each (the B1 rate in the 1937 review), about 30 GPU-min.
- Result that changes plans: if the fp-ends arm captures most of the gain, C215 gets a limitation or a narrower claim, and E61's bit budget is set by where full-precision bytes buy quality. If it captures under 30%, the fixed-r and contractive parts are real, and C215 stands.
- Novelty: C205 / A1e is E27 only, at 200M. SQ443 is a per-projection map on E27. SQ264 is a per-bit read of existing F2 cells. Closest: SQ443.

**I2. Bytes-matched frontier (quality per bit).**
- Hypothesis (spec): at equal non-embedding storage (1.58-bit ternary vs 16-bit), a ternary model with more parameters beats a narrower full-precision model in clean80 bpc.
- Cheapest test: 20M tokens, two byte-matched pairs, two seeds, widths chosen so non-embedding bytes match, FLOPs reported. About 12 runs, roughly 40 GPU-min (B1 rate). Note that at equal bytes the ternary side has about 10x the parameters, so the FLOP difference is large and must be reported.
- Result that changes plans: if ternary wins at equal bytes, the pillar is a memory argument and E61 is sized in bytes. If full precision wins, ternary is kept only if its speed case returns: C14 is WRONG FOR OUR CODE (BitLinear is slower, K2), so that case is closed for now.
- Novelty: SQ264 reads the existing cells per bit, but trains nothing at matched bytes. SQ371 is a computed memory table. SQ378 packs bytes on CPU without a quality arm.

**I3. Swap cost by overlap band (recall or ternary compensation).**
- Hypothesis (spec): the in-corpus cost of the first-layer swap grows with K9 overlap band if it is recall, and stays flat if the first layer compensates ternary error.
- Cheapest test: CPU only, the 15 SQ439 checkpoints, clean80 windows split into the SQ410 overlap bands, swap cost per band for A2f, A2s and E27. Roughly 1-2 CPU-hours (spec).
- Result that changes plans: rising with overlap means SQ442 tests the recall reading. Flat means precision work (SQ443, SQ444) comes before any memorisation argument in E61.
- Novelty: incremental. SQ410 has overlap bands but on arm-ratio NLL, not the swap. SQ441 has the swap but no bands.

**I4. Remove the prelude block from A2s.**
- Hypothesis (spec): A2s without its full-precision prelude block (embedding feeds inject directly) loses under 1%, so the prelude is not what C215 buys.
- Cheapest test: 20M tokens, three seeds, one arm, against A2s at 20M, about 11 GPU-min.
- Result that changes plans: if the loss is over 3%, the prelude is the cheapest full-precision spend. If under 1%, removing it saves 1.77M full-precision parameters, about 3.5 of 9.9 MB (computed, section 2.2), roughly 35% of A2s's storage.
- Novelty: SQ39 and SQ271 vary prelude depth at matched params, and SQ444 tries to revive the ternary prelude. None removes the prelude in A2s.

**I5. Full-precision to ternary conversion (QAT fine-tune from A2f).**
- Hypothesis (spec): a short ternary QAT phase over the last 10% of tokens, starting from the A2f checkpoint, recovers at least half of C207's ternary cost (the A2f vs A2s gap, about 9.5%).
- Cheapest test: continue the existing 200M A2f checkpoints (seeds 2-4) for 20M tokens, ternary on vs continued full precision (control). Six runs, about 20 GPU-min.
- Result that changes plans: if it recovers half, ternary becomes a conversion step and E61 can train in full precision and convert, which changes compute and risk. If not, ternary cost is trained in and the conversion route is closed.
- Novelty: A1f is QAT from 50% of steps, and its late-start effect is near null (SQ438 cites it). SQ290 is PTQ without QAT. SQ339 is eval-only. Closest: A1f.

## 4. The one experiment I would run next

A single 20M batch that puts both bit questions in one readout: F-6, F-6 with full-precision ends, A2s, and A2s without its prelude block, three seeds each (12 runs, about 42 GPU-min at the B1 rate). It separates C215's bundle into precision scope (I1) and prelude carrying (I4), and it prices each full-precision byte, which no current claim does.

- **Prediction (spec):** F-6 with full-precision ends recovers 50-100% of A2s's gain over F-6. Removing the prelude costs 1-3%.
- **Decision rule (spec, fixed before the run):** if the fp-ends arm recovers at least 70% of the gain, C215 gets a limitation event now and a narrower claim. If the prelude-removal loss is over 3%, the prelude's 1.77M full-precision parameters stay in E61's plan; if under 1%, they are dropped.
- **Caveats (spec):** 20M tokens, one LR (6e-3, the F-6 LR), so the two A2 arms are not LR-bracketed (C206's 3e-3 finding is the warning). Scale may not transfer to 200M. Register as a side quest with no ledger status until a 200M check is set, per AGENTS 0b.
