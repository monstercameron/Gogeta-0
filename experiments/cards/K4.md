# K4: does the ternary gap shrink with model size at our scale?

## Question and prediction
- **Question:** does the val-loss gap between ternary (BitLinear) and full precision shrink as d grows?
- **Prediction (committed before running):** `experiments/checks/PREDICTIONS.md`, section "K4", commit `f1b4ebd`: shrinks (e.g. ~12% → ~8% → ~5%), all gaps positive.
- **Pass criterion:** C17's local analogue is VERIFIED if the mean relative gap strictly decreases from d=64 to d=128 to d=256. The 3B-parity part stays UNVERIFIED.

## How to run it
- `wsl -d Ubuntu-24.04 -- bash -c "cd /mnt/c/Users/mreca/Desktop/Gogeta-0 && ~/.venvs/gogeta/bin/python experiments/checks/k4_ternary_gap_vs_size.py"` (env `K4_STEPS` overrides the steps)
- GPU, about 12.6 min (758 s), 12 runs. Data: `~/fusiondance-data/datasets/code-py-v1/{train,val}.bin`. HEAD `ea3aa5b` (dirty, script uncommitted).

## Runs
Prelude `[gdn, attn]` (no loop), heads = d/64, MLP = 3d, vocab 8192, T=512, batch 64, 600 steps (19.7M tokens), lr 3e-3, warm-up 100, cosine. Random train windows from `np.random.default_rng(seed)`, with the same order for fp and ternary. Val: the first 64 non-overlapping 512-token windows of val.bin.

## Results
- Mean relative gap: **15.32% → 13.76% → 10.55%**. It strictly decreases and also decreases within each seed. All gaps are positive.
- **C17 (local):** `VERIFIED`. The ledger status is at best `PARTIAL`, since 3B parity is untested.

## How to reuse this
- Template for any fp-vs-quantised A/B at matched data order (e.g. SQ21-style post-training quantisation, or other BitLinear variants).
- The size sweep can be extended to d=384 or 512 to see whether the trend continues at E-scale widths.

## Caveats
- The gaps are larger than predicted (15/14/11% vs ~12/8/5%). The shrink is modest: about 1.5× from d=64 to 256.
- Short runs (600 steps), not tuned separately for ternary (same LR). BitNet recipes often use a higher LR for ternary.
- d=64 uses 1 head; heads vary with d.
