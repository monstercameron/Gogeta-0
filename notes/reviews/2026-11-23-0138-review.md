## 2. Critique

### 2a. Architecture (gogeta/model.py)

1. **GDN is a bundle, so no result credits a mechanism.** A GDN block changes the delta-rule write, the decay gate `g`, the write strength β, three short causal convs on q/k/v, a SiLU output gate and an output RMSNorm (model.py:170–203, code-read). Every "GDN helps" result (E40/E41, E11 vs E1, C43 parity) tests the whole bundle. The README's "linear-time state" pitch and C18/C43 wording therefore assign the advantage to the state update without a test that isolates it.

### 2b. Method

2. **Document-boundary confound (new; partly verified).** train.py draws random length-(T+1) windows from one flat token stream (train.py:61–65, code-read); validation windows are fixed slices of the same stream (train.py:67–74, code-read). I found no document mask and no state reset in train.py. Attention and GDN therefore see across document boundaries, and SWA-256 and GDN see different amounts of the previous document. The arms do not get the same context distribution, and the mixer decides how much of it is used. C101 records a large window-start effect (loss 1.060 on the first 64 positions of a window vs 0.576 after, quoted). Unverified: whether tools/prepare_data.py writes `<|eos|>` between documents (train.py uses eos only in generation, lines 216–225). If it does not, every mixer comparison, including Phase 2b and the F2 factorial, shares a packing that treats the arms differently.
3. **The locality prior is not separated from the mixer.** E1's edge is largest at d=256 and on short files (C193, quoted). A dense transformer with a 256-token sliding window would carry the same prior. No arm puts SWA-256 into the E10 control, so "linear-time mixer" and "local attention" are confounded at small width.
4. **Ternary is an untested leg.** The README's "fuse the three" is the pitch. The only positive-looking LM result is C197, and SQ308 has just weakened it. Loops have no positive LM result (C6, C20). The fusion therefore has no tested combination, and the final-model plan gates the ternary leg on a result that does not exist yet.
5. **Look-then-confirm with underpowered confirmation.** SQ301 chose LRs on the seeds it reports (acknowledged in the ledger limitations). SQ307, the fresh-seed check, has a CI that spans zero. The pattern is a sign found on selected seeds, then a confirmation too small to settle it. Confirmation arms need a power calculation at the stated effect size, registered before the run.
6. **Compute and seeds.** E32, E33 and E34 each have one seed at 200M. The minimal-tier E40–E48 decomposition has one seed. Headline sentences in the README carry qualifiers, but the table rows do not repeat them.

## 3. Alternative ideas

Each is speculation (spec) until run. Costs are estimates at our 13.8M scale.

**I1. Document-boundary control.**
- Hypothesis (spec): part of the E1–E10 gap and of C193's length effect is a packing artifact, because cross-document context is used differently by full attention, SWA and GDN.
- Cheapest test: (CPU, minutes) confirm `<|eos|>` separators in prepare_data. (GPU, about 25 min) E10 and E1 at d=384, 20M tokens, seeds 0–1, at their K7 LRs (3e-3, 6e-3), with the current packing vs document-masked packing (block-diagonal attention mask; GDN state reset at document start, via fla's varlen interface if it supports it; check the API).
- Changes plans: a gap move of 0.5 points or more, or a sign flip, means every mixer pair is re-scored under masking before any further mixer claim. No move clears the packing and turns C101's window effect into a recorded limitation.
- Novelty: not queued. SQ309 (length-bucketed sampling) changes which documents are sampled, not what context a window may use.

**I2. Delta-rule ablation inside the GDN bundle.**
- Hypothesis (spec): at small width, GDN's advantage comes from the gate, short convs and output norm, not from the delta-rule write.
- Cheapest test: E1 with its GDN blocks replaced by a gated linear attention mixer with the same gate, convs, output gate and norm (no delta write), matched params; 20M tokens at d=256 and d=384, 2 seeds. About 30 GPU-min. Check which fla kernels provide a gated linear mixer without the delta rule.
- Changes plans: if the gated variant matches GDN at d=256, the delta-rule credit comes out of the README and C18/C43; if GDN wins, the write rule is the mechanism and E11 (negative eigenvalues) matters more.
- Novelty: E40 removes recurrence entirely and SQ8/SQ55 test gates. Neither isolates the write rule.

**I3. Locality-prior control on the Llama control.**
- Hypothesis (spec): a dense Llama control with a 256-token sliding-window mask on all six layers reproduces most of E1's d=256 edge, so the mixer does not matter at small width.
- Cheapest test: E10 with a sliding-window mask (check that the installed transformers version supports this on LlamaForCausalLM, else pass a 4D mask), against dense E10 and E1, at d=256, 20M tokens, 2 seeds, tuned LRs. About 25 GPU-min.
- Changes plans: if SWA-Llama matches E1, the small-width hybrid edge is a mask and the linear-mixer pitch has no support at d=256.
- Novelty: E41 is SWA-only inside FusionDance at minimal tier with the gogeta init. No SWA arm exists against the Llama control at any width.

**I4. MTP auxiliary heads on E1.**
- Hypothesis (spec): adding heads that predict t+2 to t+4 (loss weight 0.1–0.3) costs under 1% on t+1 clean80 bpc at 20M, and yields a drafter for goal 4 without a second model.
- Cheapest test: E1 vs E1+MTP, 20M tokens, 2 seeds; score t+1 clean80 and the teacher-forced acceptance of t+2 (inference). About 15–20 GPU-min.
- Changes plans: a t+1 cost above 1% removes MTP from the self-speculation path; neutrality makes SQ298 (drafter acceptance, registered in v0.4.85) cheaper to act on.
- Novelty: partial. SQ298 measures acceptance only; the base-loss cost of an auxiliary objective is not queued.

**I5. Per-token-class split of the E1–E10 gap (inference only).**
- Hypothesis (spec): the d=256 edge sits mostly in identifiers that repeat within a file (copy and locality), not in syntax or whitespace.
- Cheapest test: per-token NLL on clean80 for the existing 7-seed E1 and E10 checkpoints (GPU scoring minutes, or CPU on about 200 docs); split tokens into first-occurrence identifiers, repeated identifiers, keywords, indentation and literals.
- Changes plans: an edge concentrated in repeated identifiers points to in-context copying (link SQ240); an edge in syntax points to a local n-gram prior and weakens the long-context reading of C193.
- Novelty: SQ240 is a synthetic copy probe and SQ194 is a formatting test. A per-class split on real checkpoints is not queued.

**I6. Ternary price by post-training quantisation on the E1 stack (inference only).**
- Hypothesis (spec): absmean ternarising a trained E1 without QAT costs more than 20% clean80 bpc, so the E3 budget must assume QAT training.
- Cheapest test: quantise the 7-seed E1 checkpoints (absmean weights, int8 activations as in BitLinear) and score clean80. Minutes of GPU.
- Changes plans: a cost under 3% makes E3 cheap and lets the ternary gate in the final-model plan be decided early; a cost over 20% puts QAT cost into the M4 estimate.
- Novelty: SQ21 covers INT8, INT4 and ternary references on the E2 core only. The non-looped E1 stack is not covered.

## 4. The single experiment I would run next

**I1, the document-boundary control**, in two steps.

- **Step 0 (CPU, under 15 min, no GPU):** read tools/prepare_data.py and confirm whether documents are separated by `<|eos|>` in train.bin and whether windows cross those separators. Register the result as a check before any GPU step.
- **Step 1 (one GPU slot, about 25 min, only after registration per AGENTS §0 and §6):** E10 and E1 at d=384, 20M tokens, seeds 0–1, at their K7 LRs (3e-3, 6e-3), under the current packing and under document-masked packing. Prediction (spec): the E1−E10 gap moves by less than 0.5 points. Decision: a move of 0.5 points or more, or a sign flip, re-scores every mixer pair under masking before further mixer claims; no move clears the packing for those pairs.

Why this one: it is the cheapest test that could invalidate headline comparisons across every arm, and it fits the one-GPU-slot budget. The F2 factorial is in its blinded stage and shares this packing. Changing the packing for F2 mid-stage would be a registered deviation, not a silent change, so the step runs on its own arms. Whether fla's varlen interface supports the masked route is unverified (spec); check the API in Step 0.
