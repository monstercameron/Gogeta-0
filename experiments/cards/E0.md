# E0: Dense transformer baseline

## Question and prediction
- **Question:** what does a plain ~10M-non-emb causal transformer reach on `code-py-v1` at our budget?
- **Prediction:** registry `experiments/experiments.json` → E0.prediction (committed in `0dc7491`, before the run). Loss range 0.90–1.30: **missed** (0.606).
