## 2. Critique

**2.1 Units differ between the headline and the matched readout (computed, quoted).** The matched script says "log scale" (a2_compute_matched.py l.4), and its per-seed values are ln-ratios (-0.1645, -0.1589). Converted to a ratio, the matched gap is about -14.9% with CI about [-17.9%, -11.8%] (computed). C209 quotes "-16.67%" with a % sign and does not state the scale in the claim text. Before the README quotes the matched number, both should be put on one scale. Either way, the matched gap appears about 1.5 to 2 points smaller than the headline (computed), which is the size the 10% compute gap could plausibly explain (spec on magnitude).

**2.2 Shared weights take summed gradients at one global LR (code-read).** In `FusionDance.forward` the core's weights are applied r times, and autograd sums their gradients over passes (bptt_loops caps the graph; with r=2 both passes are backpropagated). A shared block therefore takes a larger effective step per unique parameter than an unshared block at the same LR. The LR bracket (3e-3 to 1.2e-2) tunes one global LR, so it cannot separate "sharing costs quality" from "sharing changes the step size". This bears on A2f vs E24 (+3.2%, C204 P4), the loop cost that decides D3. Not isolated anywhere in the registry I read (SQ12 is a global LR stress test).

**2.3 The compute-matched E27 is not data-matched or LR-bracketed (quoted).** E27_cm sees 221.6M tokens against A2s's 200M, so it is FLOP-matched but not data-matched. I did not find an LR bracket for E27_cm in its readout; check the config before C209's compute claim is upgraded.

**2.4 Clean80 is one threshold, chosen after K9 (quoted).** clean80 (<80% 13-gram overlap) was defined after K9's 41.7% near-duplicate finding. F2 has clean50 and dup80 analyses (INDEX: f2_analysis_clean50, f2_analysis_dup80). I found no equivalent threshold check for the A2/A3 headlines (C206-C209 cite only clean80). A sensitivity check would show whether 16.7% and 3.3% move with the threshold.

**2.5 Three seeds, df 2, and post hoc multiplicity (computed, quoted).** The per-seed values -17.05, -16.12 and -16.82 give a sample sd of about 0.48 points, so the df-2 interval is set by three numbers. A2's six comparisons were Holm-corrected after the fact (a2_holm.json), not registered as a family. The seed-to-seed sd of a fresh-seed arm at 20M or 200M is not measured anywhere in the registry I read; SQ232 measured only same-seed replay spread.

**2.6 "Matched compute" and ternary (code-read, already triaged).** `flops_per_token` has no ternary term (BitLinear is counted at full precision), and no-grad passes are counted forward-only (train.py:345-346, per v0.5.19). Every ternary "at matched compute" is quality at equal full-precision FLOPs. The measured ternary gain is bytes (C208 44.0 MB; SQ378 16x packing). Goal 6 has no speed evidence yet (K2 / C14: BitLinear slower in eager mode). The README status line should say this where it states the headline.

**2.7 Byte-track units are asserted, not checked (quoted, code-read).** A4 is scored as nats per target token, with the byte decoder's EOT counted (a4_fullstack.py l.5-6), and the script says the two arms are comparable on the same tokens. I did not check the target alignment in code. It is a cheap audit item before SQ387 reports.

## 3. Alternative ideas (all speculation; none is in side-quests.md or the queue)

**I-A. Optimizer confound of sharing (spec).**
- Hypothesis: part of the shared-vs-unshared gap (A2f vs E24, +3.2%) is the larger effective step of shared weights, not their function. A per-group LR multiplier on the shared core closes some of it.
- Cheapest test: 20M tokens, 2 seeds, full precision (A2f-style). Shared 6x2 with core LR multiplier 0.5, 1 and 2 at the same global LR, vs unshared 12 blocks at that LR. 8 runs, about 35 GPU-min; one campaign file.
- Change of plans: if the best multiplier closes more than half the gap, re-read A2f vs E24 at its best core LR before D3 is decided. If the best is 1 and the gap persists, the loop penalty holds at 20M and D3 stays open.
- Novelty: none found (grep for per-group, core LR, gradient scale returns nothing; SQ12 is global).

**I-B. Pass disagreement as a free halting signal (spec; extends SQ403).**
- Hypothesis: per-token KL between the r=1 and r=2 readouts of A2s predicts how much pass 2 lowers the loss, giving a computable halting feature for goal 3 and E5 without a trained head.
- Cheapest test: inference only, A2s seeds 2-4, in the same eval run as SQ403. Per-token KL(p_r1 || p_r2) and loss(r1) minus loss(r2) on clean80 tokens; Spearman rho. Minutes on GPU; CPU on a subset.
- Change of plans: rho of 0.3 or more supports building E5's halting head on this feature. Rho near 0 means halting has to be learned, so E5 stays a training experiment.
- Novelty: SQ92 is the oracle bound (best r per token), not a computable signal. SQ403 reports loss by r only.

**I-C. Temporal holdout (spec; needs human approval for any download, AGENTS §0a).**
- Hypothesis: the A2 gain persists on code written after the corpus snapshot, a cut that n-gram overlap (SQ99, SQ379, SQ400) only approximates.
- Cheapest test: local Python files created after the corpus build date, if any exist (check mtimes; the count may be small). Otherwise a human-approved fetch of post-snapshot code. Score A2s and E27 with the clean80 protocol.
- Change of plans: if the gain falls below 5% on post-snapshot code, the assembly gain is memorisation-sensitive and C213 moves toward refutation.
- Novelty: SQ99, SQ379 and SQ400 use an overlap threshold; a time cut has no threshold to tune.

**I-D. Threshold sensitivity of the A-series headlines (spec; extends F2's clean50/dup80).**
- Hypothesis: C206 (-3.3%), C208 and C209 keep their sign and rough size at clean50, clean80 and the full v2 val.
- Cheapest test: re-score the saved A2/A3 checkpoints, about 5 GPU-min per arm; CPU only if per-document losses are already saved.
- Change of plans: a sign flip, or a change above 30% at clean50, means the headline depends on the threshold, and the README needs a threshold caveat.
- Novelty: F2 has clean50 and dup80 analyses; the registry has none for A2 or A3.

**I-E. Seed-variance floor (spec).**
- Hypothesis: the seed sd of a fresh-seed arm at 20M is close to the 3-seed pooled sd the A-series uses, so the df-2 intervals (C5, C197, C206-C209) are not much optimistic.
- Cheapest test: the E1 config at 20M tokens, 8 seeds, one LR. About 25 GPU-min. Compare the 8-seed sd with the 3-seed sd and recompute the df-2 interval.
- Change of plans: if the 8-seed sd is more than 1.5 times the 3-seed pooled sd, the registered intervals widen and the 3-seed VERIFIED claims need an n of 8 recheck.
- Novelty: SQ232 measured same-seed, same-SKU replay spread (0.0022 on E10), not across-seed sd of a fresh-seed arm.
