- Recipe-level claim only: the A1 one-change effects behind the stack are exploratory (2 seeds) and confounded inside it. Single horizon (200M; SQ380 tests 600M), in-corpus clean80 only (SQ379 out of corpus). A2f vs E24 shows the assembled loop model still trails an unshared GDN stack by 3.2%. [event 3, v0.5.18]
- The fixed-r comparator check (event 8, A2s vs A1a -12.56%) is exploratory: post hoc, unregistered, unpaired across different seed sets (A1a seeds 0-1 are A1's selection seeds). It bounds how much of the headline the fixed-r choice could explain; it is not a confirmatory test. [event 9, v0.5.40]
- Not parameter-matched: A2s has about 15.1M unique non-embedding params vs E27 and A1a about 9.5M (the A1g 6x2 shape adds unique params by design). The -16.7% (and -12.6% vs A1a) therefore includes a unique-parameter effect, which C197 says is large at matched FLOPs, so 'mostly the assembly' (v0.5.39) is not shown. SQ419 (F-6: E27's recipe on the 6-block core) separates shape and unique params from the compensations. [event 10, v0.5.42]
- Decomposed by C1 (v0.5.56): most of the gap (S = 0.61 [0.57, 0.65]) is the 6-block core shape and its 1.6x unique parameters, not the compensations; the compensations' part is C215 (about -6.9% at matched shape, params and FLOPs). [event 14, v0.5.56]
